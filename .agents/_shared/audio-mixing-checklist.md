# Audio Mixing Checklist

Classification: `Core`

Purpose:
provide a reusable audio quality gate for future `Why It Works` videos before review or final renders.

Use this after voiceover is approved and before final render review.

## Core Rule

`If the viewer cannot clearly hear David23, the mix fails.`

Music and sound effects should support dry humor, not compete with English learner clarity.

## 1. Pre-Mix Setup

```text
[ ] final or review voiceover is selected
[ ] narration timing is locked enough for audio review
[ ] candidate music source and license are documented
[ ] sound effects are self-made, generated, licensed, or otherwise safe
[ ] all audio files are local to the future video HyperFrames project before render
```

## 2. Narration Clarity

```text
[ ] every spoken sentence is understandable on laptop speakers
[ ] every spoken sentence is understandable on headphones
[ ] key English phrases are not masked by music or effects
[ ] dense explanation sections are easy to follow
[ ] punchline lines have enough space to feel deadpan
[ ] captions support the voice, but are not required to understand it
```

Immediate fixes:

- lower music
- remove effects under words
- duck music during dense lines
- add near-silence before or after punchlines
- simplify the audio bed

## 3. Music Selection Gate

```text
[ ] tested `3` candidate tracks under the first `30` seconds
[ ] tested selected track under one dense explanation section
[ ] rejected tracks that sound corporate, cinematic, motivational, or too emotional
[ ] selected track is loopable without becoming annoying
[ ] selected track does not make the narration feel rushed
[ ] selected track supports dry humor instead of telling the viewer how to feel
```

Reject the track if it sounds like:

- a startup ad
- a finance course
- a documentary trailer
- a motivational video
- luxury brand background music
- generic corporate explainer music

## 4. Music Mix Gate

```text
[ ] music is lower than narration at all times
[ ] music ducks during dense explanation
[ ] music ducks or disappears for dry punchlines when needed
[ ] no bass or percussion masks consonants
[ ] loop points do not call attention to themselves
[ ] the mix still works when played quietly
```

Working standard:

`Music should be felt more than noticed.`

## 5. Sound Effect Gate

```text
[ ] every effect has a clear joke, reveal, or action reason
[ ] no effect is used only because a board changed
[ ] effects are short and quiet
[ ] effects land on or just after the visual cue
[ ] effects do not cover important words
[ ] repeated effects are intentional, not habit
[ ] no dramatic impacts, loud whooshes, or cartoon overload
```

Remove the effect if:

- the joke works without it
- the line becomes harder to hear
- it makes the video feel childish
- it draws attention to editing instead of meaning

## 6. Silence And Punchline Gate

```text
[ ] at least some punchlines are allowed to land with silence or near-silence
[ ] awkward WIT reactions are not over-scored
[ ] suspicious labels can be read without audio clutter
[ ] deadpan lines are not forced by a funny sound
```

Silence is acceptable.
Over-explaining jokes with audio is not.

## 7. English Learner Gate

```text
[ ] intermediate English learners can follow the voice without straining
[ ] important repeated phrases are easy to hear
[ ] unfamiliar or useful words are not hidden under effects
[ ] slower narration moments stay calm, not empty
[ ] the final mix does not make the video feel faster than the script
```

If learner clarity and sound design conflict, learner clarity wins.

## 8. Playback Checks

Check the same cut in at least two conditions:

```text
[ ] laptop speakers or small desktop speakers
[ ] headphones or earbuds
[ ] low playback volume
[ ] normal playback volume
```

Optional but useful:

```text
[ ] phone speaker check
[ ] watch the first `30` seconds without looking at the timeline
[ ] watch one dense explanation section without looking at captions
```

## 9. HyperFrames Render Check

```text
[ ] audio files are local, not remote URLs
[ ] voice, music, and effects start at intended times
[ ] no missing media warnings affect audio
[ ] exported review render includes all intended audio layers
[ ] render start and end do not cut off voice or tail audio
```

## 10. Pass/Fail Decision

Pass only if:

```text
[ ] narration is always the clearest layer
[ ] music does not make the video feel like an ad
[ ] effects land jokes without clutter
[ ] dense sections remain learner-friendly
[ ] punchlines have enough dry space
[ ] source/licensing notes are documented for future production
```

Final note:

`The best Why It Works mix sounds like the video is quietly amused by the problem, not trying to sell the solution.`
