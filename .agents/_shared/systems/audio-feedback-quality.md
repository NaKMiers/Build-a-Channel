# Audio, Feedback, And Quality System

Classification: `Core production system`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Use this file for music, sound effects, audio mixing, production gates, final review, post-upload review, and reusable learning.

## Audio Rule

Narration is the product. Music and sound effects are support.

Music and effects must never make spoken English harder to understand.

## Music

Use:

- simple loopable beds
- low-drama tracks
- quiet suspicious or dry background energy
- near-silence for some punchlines

Avoid:

- corporate explainer music
- cinematic trailer music
- motivational tracks
- loud impacts
- constant whooshes
- effects that cover words
- unclear licensed audio

## Sound Effects

Good effect families:

- red marker scribble
- receipt printer
- notification ping
- tiny payment sound
- lock click
- paper movement
- timer tick
- quiet reveal

Rules:

- short
- quiet
- purposeful
- never stacked on dense narration
- no cartoon overload

## Mix Checklist

Before final review:

- narration is clear on laptop speakers
- music sits under narration
- dense explanations have less music or none
- punchlines have room
- effects do not hit over important words
- captions and audio timing feel aligned
- no clipping, harshness, or distracting volume jumps

## Production Quality Gates

Use these lightweight gates:

1. Topic angle passes the angle score.
2. Packaging has a clear click promise.
3. First `10` seconds pays off the promise.
4. Script is clear, funny, and learner-friendly.
5. Boards are readable when paused.
6. Voiceover matches the approved narrator direction.
7. Audio mix keeps narration first.
8. Final upload package is honest and consistent.
9. Post-upload review captures one useful lesson.

Hard fails:

- misleading claim
- unreadable thumbnail or labels
- unclear first `10` seconds
- copied creator layout
- music or effects hide narration
- viewer cannot understand the point if one joke is missed

## Final Review

Review:

- package match
- first `10` seconds
- retention dips
- paused-frame clarity
- WIT emotion
- real-life asset safety
- learner-friendly clarity
- audio clarity
- upload metadata

Pass only when the video feels clear, honest, readable, and worth watching.

## Post-Upload Review

When a future video is published, create one short review when analytics or useful qualitative feedback are available.

Track what is available:

- impressions
- CTR
- views after `24h` and `7d`
- average view duration
- average percentage viewed
- first `30s` retention
- retention dips
- traffic source
- comments
- repeated confusion
- subs gained
- production effort

If data is too small, use qualitative signals:

- thumbnail clarity
- first `10` seconds strength
- viewer feedback
- learner clarity
- production difficulty

## Learning Rule

Measure the upload. Learn one useful thing. Change the next video.

Promote only reusable lessons to `.agents/_shared/channel/learning-log.md`.

Use labels:

- `Operational lesson`
- `Experiment result`
- `Packaging lesson`
- `Audience insight`

Confidence labels:

- High: repeated signal or strong evidence
- Medium: useful but still needs more examples
- Low: interesting observation, not yet a rule

Do not rewrite core strategy from one upload without explicit user confirmation.
