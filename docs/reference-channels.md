# Reference Channels

This file captures the channels used as core references for `Why It Works`.

All subscriber counts and observations below were checked during research on `2026-05-19`.

## How To Use These References

Study these channels for:

- Topic selection
- Hook writing
- Structure
- Humor density
- Thumbnail logic
- Pacing
- Audience fit

Do not copy them line for line.

## 1. Mèo Giải Thích

- URL: [Mèo Giải Thích](https://www.youtube.com/@M%C3%A8oGi%E1%BA%A3iTh%C3%ADch)
- Observed size: `113K subscribers`
- Why it matters:
  Strong question-led explainers, clear structure, broad social and economic framing, no-face presentation, and good “explain one thing well” discipline.

### Best Study Videos

- `Giải thích về kinh tế cho người chưa biết gì trong 18 phút`
  Good reference for teaching a large topic in a simple way.
- `Vì sao học sinh lúc nào cũng ghét đi học?`
  Good reference for turning a relatable human problem into a strong explainer.

## 4. Half as Interesting

- URL: [Half as Interesting](https://www.youtube.com/@halfasinteresting)
- Observed size: `2.93M subscribers`
- Why it matters:
  One of the best examples of weird-question explainer content in English. Strong hooks, smart brevity, and a voice that stays informative without getting stiff.

### Best Study Videos

- `Why Saudi Arabia Imports Sand`
  Great example of curiosity-first topic selection.
- `The Ancient, Strange Coding Language that 95% of ATMs Use`
  Great example of turning a hidden system into a compelling explainer.

## 5. Casually Explained

- URL: [Casually Explained](https://www.youtube.com/@CasuallyExplained)
- Observed size: `4.19M subscribers`
- Why it matters:
  Strong no-face English channel with a highly memorable voice. Very useful for learning how to sound like a person while still explaining things clearly.

### Best Study Videos

- `Casually Explained: Levels of Wealth`
  Strong study piece for social commentary with humor.
- `Casually Explained: Wallstreetbets`
  Strong study piece for funny explanation around money and internet culture.
- `Casually Explained: The English Language`
  Strong study piece for making language itself funny without becoming a formal lesson. Useful for `Why It Works` because the video proves that simple English, dry jokes, pronunciation jokes, and cultural comparison can attract both native viewers and English learners.

## 6. OverSimplified

- URL: [OverSimplified](https://www.youtube.com/@OverSimplified)
- Observed size: `9.52M subscribers`
- Why it matters:
  Best reference here for pacing, visual payoff, running jokes, and keeping long explanations entertaining.

### Best Study Videos

- `WW2 - OverSimplified (Part 1)`
  Strong for large-scale topic framing and joke rhythm.
- `The Napoleonic Wars - OverSimplified (Part 1)`
  Strong for long-form narrative pacing.

## Shared Lessons Across All References

- Lead with a question people already half-feel but cannot explain
- Focus on one idea per video
- Use humor to support clarity
- Keep language simple
- Make the English easy enough for learners without sounding like a lesson
- Make the topic feel relevant to daily life
- End with a satisfying insight, not just more facts
