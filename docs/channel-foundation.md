# Channel Foundation

## Channel Identity

- Channel name: `Why It Works`
- Primary language: `English`
- Format: `no-face explainer channel`
- Core promise: `Explain money, the internet, and modern life in a way that's actually fun.`
- Core feel: `smart topics, simple English, dry humor, high curiosity`

## Positioning

This should not be a generic finance channel, a generic coding channel, or a generic self-improvement channel.

The strongest positioning is:

`A funny English explainer channel about money, internet behavior, society, business, and weird modern life.`

The channel should help viewers feel:

- `I finally understand this`
- `This is smarter than normal internet content`
- `This is funny without being stupid`
- `This creator sounds like a real person, not a textbook`

## Target Audience

- Young adults
- Students
- Early-career workers
- Internet-native viewers
- People curious about money, business, social behavior, and hidden systems
- Viewers who like clever explainers but do not want lecture-style content

## Channel Promise

`Why It Works` will turn confusing or annoying parts of modern life into clear, entertaining explanations.

The channel should answer questions like:

- Why does this happen?
- Who benefits from this?
- Why does modern life feel like this?
- Why do people behave this way?
- Why is this system so weird?

## What Makes This Work

The strongest reference channels all share these traits:

- Strong curiosity hooks
- One clear question per video
- Clear explanation without academic tone
- A human voice instead of corporate or textbook voice
- Humor used as support, not as the whole product
- Packaging hard topics as entertainment

## Best Style Mix

- `Half as Interesting` for topic selection
- `Casually Explained` for tone and phrasing
- `Mèo Giải Thích` for explanation structure
- `Vui Vẻ` for energy and packaging
- `OverSimplified` for pacing, payoff, and memorable storytelling

This should be treated as inspiration, not imitation.

## What Not To Copy Too Literally

- OverSimplified's heavy production workload
- Casually Explained's exact persona
- Mèo Giải Thích's exact topical framing
- Vui Vẻ's broad sprawl across too many categories

The goal is to learn the strengths and build a distinct identity.

## Topic Selection Rules

Good topics usually create one of these reactions:

- `Wait, that's true`
- `I never thought about it like that`
- `This annoys me too`
- `I know the symptom, but not the reason`

Preferred video frames:

- `Why X happens`
- `How X actually works`
- `Why X is broken`
- `The strange logic behind X`

## Voice And Tone

The voice should be:

- Clear
- Conversational
- Slightly dry
- Slightly skeptical
- Funny without trying too hard
- Smart without sounding elite

The voice should not be:

- Academic
- Overly motivational
- Hyper-shouty
- Fake-deep
- Overly corporate

## Visual Direction

- No face on screen
- Simple 2D illustrations
- Bold flat colors
- Strong silhouettes
- Expressive stick-figure or mascot-like characters
- Simple backgrounds
- Clean motion that supports the joke or explanation

Thumbnail direction:

- 3 to 6 words max
- One visual idea
- Strong contrast
- Clear emotion or contradiction

## Recommended Channel Formula

Each video should follow this structure:

1. Hook
2. Reframe
3. Explain in 3 to 5 chunks
4. Add humor throughout
5. End with the real payoff

## Script Formula

### 1. Hook

Open with a strong question, contradiction, or painful truth.

Example:

`Here's the fun part about being an adult: everything is a subscription except your salary.`

### 2. Reframe

Explain what the topic is really about.

Example:

`This isn't just about Netflix. It's about how modern life quietly turned into rent.`

### 3. The 3-Part Explanation

Use a clean structure:

- What people think
- What's actually happening
- Why it keeps happening

### 4. Humor Beat Every 20 To 40 Seconds

Use:

- Sarcastic contrast
- Exaggeration
- Fake dialogue
- Absurd examples
- Simple analogies

### 5. Payoff

End with the real lesson or insight.

The ending should make the viewer feel:

- `Now I get it`
- `That explains a lot`
- `This is bigger than I thought`

### 6. Outro

Keep it short.

Example:

`If modern life keeps inventing new ways to be ridiculous, we'll keep explaining them.`

## Repeatable Script Template

```text
[HOOK]
Here's something ridiculous: _____.

[REFRAME]
Most people think this is about _____.
It's not.
It's actually about _____.

[POINT 1]
First, _____.
This matters because _____.

[JOKE OR ANALOGY]
This is basically like _____.

[POINT 2]
Second, _____.
This is where things get stupid.

[POINT 3]
Third, _____.
And this is why the problem never really goes away.

[PAYOFF]
So the real reason _____ is not _____.
It's _____.

[OUTRO]
If you want more videos about money, the internet, and modern life, stick around.
```

- `3 shorts per week` cut from the long video
- Batch script ideas in groups of 4 or more
- Reuse one consistent voice and thumbnail logic
