## Direction

This pass makes Wit:

- shorter, cuter, and more compact than v1
- creator-inspired through black glasses, soft dark parted hair, calm expression, and a striped shirt
- slightly glittery simple eyes with clean highlights
- simple enough to simplify later for Remotion animation
- tied to the channel through the receipt-like tie

## Prompt

```text
Edit the provided WIT character image while preserving the same character identity and overall style.

Primary request: Make WIT cuter and shorter, with more compact chibi proportions. Keep him recognizable as the same WIT from the reference: messy dark parted hair, large black round glasses, warm cheeks, small smile, pale blue striped short-sleeve shirt, dark shorts, black shoes, and the receipt-like tie.

Specific edits:
- Make the body shorter and more compact, with slightly larger head-to-body ratio.
- Make the face softer and cuter, but still handsome and smart.
- Make the eyes slightly larger and more glittery like anime eyes, but keep them simple: black oval/dot eyes with 2-3 clean white highlights, no complex iris, no dramatic anime rendering.
- Keep the glasses bold and round.
- Keep the messy dark hair shape, but make it a little softer and fluffier.
- Keep the receipt tie in the same position and style, with tiny abstract marks only and no readable words.
- Keep the warm off-white background and hand-drawn soft texture.

Constraints: Do not make him look like a different person. Do not turn it into glossy 3D, heavy anime, babyish toddler, superhero, corporate mascot, or loud colorful style. No text, no logo, no watermark. Preserve the clean 2D mascot reference look for `Why It Works`.
```
