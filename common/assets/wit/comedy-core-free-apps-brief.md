# WIT Comedy Core Free Apps Brief

Classification:
`Experiment`

Scope:
Reusable asset-generation brief for testing WIT as a comedy victim in `Why Free Apps Are Never Really Free`.

This does not replace `core-24` and does not change core channel identity.

## Purpose

WIT should become more useful as:

- a betrayed phone user
- a victim of hidden app costs
- a person physically affected by notifications, paywalls, lock-in, and subscriptions
- a readable reaction device for mobile viewers

Keep two modes:

- `Clean WIT` for calm explanation
- `Suffering WIT` for jokes, reveals, thumbnails, and retention beats

## Proposed Folder

`common/assets/wit/poses/comedy-core-free-apps`

Only create this folder when assets are actually generated or drawn.

## Asset Requirements

- transparent PNG
- consistent WIT identity
- clear full-body silhouette
- readable emotion at `25%` screen size
- no third-party app logos
- no readable brand names
- no background scene baked into the character asset
- no Casually Explained stick-figure imitation
- enough transparent margin for shake, bounce, and slight rotation

## Contact Sheet

Contact sheet after generation:

`wit-free-apps-comedy-core-contact-sheet.png`

Layout:

- `5 columns x 4 rows`
- include existing fallback poses and new comedy poses
- label each pose with filename and one emotion
- mark `Clean WIT` and `Suffering WIT` sections
- check at full size, `50%`, and `25%`

## Do Not Generate

- a full replacement WIT identity
- cute smiling versions of negative moments
- polished presenter poses for punchlines
- detailed scene backgrounds
- brand-specific app UI
- exact Casually Explained-style stick figures
