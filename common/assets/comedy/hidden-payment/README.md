# Hidden Payment Comedy Assets

Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Use this category for money, subscriptions, confusing prices, hidden fees, checkout pain, free trials, and modern-life costs.

## Category Rule

`A friendly promise becomes a physical bill.`

## Seed Targets

| Asset ID | Object | Intended format | Safe-use target | Comedy job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hidden-payment-receipt-printer` | receipt printer | transparent PNG or SVG | thumbnail and video | spits out consequences after a friendly promise |
| `hidden-payment-long-receipt` | long receipt | transparent PNG or SVG | thumbnail and video | turns a small purchase into an impossible paper trail |
| `hidden-payment-subscription-bill` | subscription bill | PNG or HTML/SVG mockup | video-only | makes monthly cost look polite and unavoidable |
| `hidden-payment-payment-failed-popup` | payment failed popup | fictional UI mockup | video-only | turns a clean screen into a tiny threat |
| `hidden-payment-price-tag-asterisk` | price tag with asterisk | transparent PNG or SVG | thumbnail and video | hides the joke in the footnote |

## Good Uses

- hide payment objects behind `FREE`, `CONVENIENT`, or `START TRIAL`
- make the object arrive after the narrator says the supposed benefit
- let WIT get buried, chased, or interrupted by the payment object
- combine with red markup only when the correction is the joke

## Do Not Use For

- real private receipts or bank data
- real company checkout screens copied exactly
- decorative money symbols that do not explain the point
- too many payment objects in one board
