# Modern Life Pain Comedy Assets

Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Use this category for work, stress, productivity, adult chores, cheap products, delivery, moving, email, calendars, and lifestyle pressure.

## Category Rule

`Normal adult life should look like a small machine built to annoy people.`

## Seed Targets

| Asset ID | Object | Intended format | Safe-use target | Comedy job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `modern-life-calendar-wall` | calendar wall | PNG, SVG, or HTML board asset | video-only | every day looks claimed by someone else |
| `modern-life-checklist-pile` | checklist pile | transparent PNG or SVG | thumbnail and video | productivity becomes physical clutter |
| `modern-life-delivery-bag` | delivery bag | transparent PNG or self-shot cutout | thumbnail and video | convenience arrives carrying the bill |
| `modern-life-broken-product` | broken product | transparent PNG or rough drawing | thumbnail and video | cheap object fails at the exact wrong time |
| `modern-life-unread-email-stack` | unread email stack | PNG, SVG, or HTML mockup | video-only | inbox becomes paperwork with a login screen |

## Good Uses

- use one relatable object as evidence for the spoken point
- make WIT small beside an adult-life object that should not be that powerful
- label the object with simple English, not a long explanation
- combine with sound effects only when the joke needs a small punctuation

## Do Not Use For

- generic office stock photos with no joke
- clutter that makes the board hard to read
- real personal schedules, emails, bills, or addresses
- motivational self-improvement imagery
