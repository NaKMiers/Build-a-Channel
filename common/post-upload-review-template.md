Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Purpose:
provide the reusable template for reviewing future `Why It Works` uploads after publishing.

## Use Rules

- Keep the filled review short.
- Do not invent analytics that are not available.
- Label low-sample conclusions as `Low confidence`.
- Do not change channel foundation from one upload.
- Promote only reusable lessons into `docs/learning-log.md`.
- Move reusable assets or workflow patterns into `common/` only when they help multiple videos.
