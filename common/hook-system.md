# Why It Works First 10 Seconds Hook System

Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Purpose:
make every future `Why It Works` video open as a curiosity event, not a polite introduction.

This system applies to future video planning, scripting, packaging, and HyperFrames production. It does not design or approve the opening for any existing video project.

## Core Standard

The first `10` seconds must prove the video is worth watching before the viewer has time to leave.

Working rule:

```text
Open with a situation, not an introduction.
```

The opening should make the viewer think:

```text
Wait, why is that normal thing suddenly suspicious?
```

Do not start with:

- channel branding
- a title card
- a slow definition
- a summary of what the video will cover
- a clean explanation before anything feels wrong

## First 10 Seconds Jobs

Every hook must do four jobs:

| Job | What It Means | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the topic | One clear object or situation tells the viewer what world we are in. | second `3` |
| Show the contradiction | Something appears that should not be there, or the promise becomes suspicious. | second `5` |
| Show WIT's emotional position | WIT reacts as the viewer's emotional shortcut. | second `8` |
| Create the bigger question | The narrator or final label turns the moment into the video's main question. | second `10` |

If the topic is still vague by second `3`, the hook is too slow.
If nothing feels suspicious by second `5`, the hook is too polite.
If WIT is neutral by second `8`, the opening loses the channel's emotional point of view.
If the thumbnail promise is not paid off by second `10`, the package and opening are disconnected.

## Default Hook Formula

Use this sequence first:

```text
normal thing -> suspicious detail -> WIT reaction -> bigger question
```

The pattern:

1. A normal object promises something easy, cheap, fast, smart, productive, free, or convenient.
2. A suspicious detail appears behind it.
3. WIT reacts to the contradiction, not to the audience in general.
4. The narrator turns the object into a bigger question about money, the internet, society, business, or modern life.

## Four-Board Opening

Default timing:

| Board | Time | Job | Visual | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Topic Object | `0:00-0:03` | Make the topic readable. | One clear object connected to the topic. | One phrase only. |
| 2. Contradiction | `0:03-0:06` | Make the object suspicious. | Something appears that should not be there. | Short correction or suspicious label. |
| 3. WIT Reaction | `0:06-0:08` | Show the human feeling. | WIT reacts to the contradiction. | Optional: `?`, `wait`, `later`, or red-marker note. |
| 4. Video Promise | `0:08-0:10` | Name the bigger question. | The full problem becomes clear. | Key phrase for the video. |

Use [first-10-seconds-board-template.md](hook-templates/first-10-seconds-board-template.md) to plan future hooks.

## Hook Types

Choose the hook type that best fits the title-thumbnail promise.

### 1. Suspicious Object

Use when the topic has one dominant object.

Pattern:

```text
object looks normal -> hidden charge/rule/trap appears -> WIT notices
```

Good for:

- apps
- subscriptions
- wallets
- carts
- price tags
- food delivery
- productivity tools
- bills

### 2. False Promise

Use when the topic is about a claim that sounds helpful but produces the opposite feeling.

Pattern:

```text
promise appears -> result contradicts it -> WIT side-eyes the promise
```

Good for:

- convenience
- passive income
- productivity advice
- discounts
- free services
- social media advice

### 3. Tiny Problem, Huge System

Use when the topic starts with a small daily annoyance and reveals a larger system.

Pattern:

```text
small annoyance -> hidden system label -> WIT realizes the problem is bigger
```

Good for:

- confusing prices
- feeling broke
- bad advice
- modern work
- product downgrades

### 4. Emotional Receipt

Use when the topic is about accumulated cost, time, attention, stress, or mental load.

Pattern:

```text
normal action -> receipt keeps printing -> WIT becomes the receipt victim
```

Good for:

- monthly payments
- hidden costs
- convenience fees
- attention economy
- mental expense

## Narration Rules

The first spoken line should:

- be simple
- be specific
- sound like a person
- contain the contradiction
- use common words before fancy words
- be understandable without seeing the title

Avoid:

- `In today's video...`
- `Have you ever wondered...`
- `This video will explain...`
- `Before we begin...`
- abstract setup before a concrete object appears

Better line shapes:

```text
This thing says it saves money, which is usually when money starts escaping.
This app says it is free, which is a brave thing to say near a credit card.
Modern life keeps offering convenience, and somehow your wallet still looks nervous.
The weird thing about [topic] is not that it exists. It is that it keeps working.
```

## Visual Rules

Use:

- one dominant object
- one visible contradiction
- one WIT emotion
- one short label per board
- hard cuts
- handwritten-looking labels
- strong mobile-readable silhouettes
- one recurring motif from the packaging system when possible

Avoid:

- complex dashboards
- multiple equal objects
- long text blocks
- neutral WIT
- branding in the first `10` seconds
- a title card before the situation
- explaining the whole mechanism
- animated clutter that only reads at full size

## WIT Rules

WIT is the viewer's emotional shortcut.

In the first `10` seconds, WIT should be:

- suspicious
- betrayed
- trapped
- panicked
- confused
- defeated
- deadpan at something obviously ridiculous

Do not use neutral presenter WIT in the hook.

Preferred hook poses:

- `wit-pose-staring-at-viewer`
- `wit-pose-suspicious-phone`
- `wit-pose-betrayed-by-phone`
- `wit-pose-financially-attacked`
- `wit-pose-buried-in-receipts`
- `wit-pose-subscription-panic`
- `wit-pose-tiny-defeated`
- `wit-pose-deadpan-side-eye`

Use the channel-wide WIT rules in [docs/branding/wit-channel-system.md](../docs/branding/wit-channel-system.md) and [common/assets/wit/usage-rules.md](assets/wit/usage-rules.md).

## Relationship To Packaging

The hook must pay off the title-thumbnail pair.

Use this chain:

```text
Thumbnail shows the weird situation.
Title names the hidden logic.
First 10 seconds proves the weird situation is real.
```

If the thumbnail shows a suspicious object, the hook should show the same object type or the same contradiction.

If the thumbnail shows WIT trapped, attacked, betrayed, or confused, the hook should explain what did that to him.

If the title promises hidden logic, the hook should create the question before explaining the logic.

## Learner-Friendly Clarity

The hook should be easy for intermediate English learners without feeling like a lesson.

Use:

- short spoken lines
- concrete nouns
- visible keywords
- repeated key phrase from the title or thumbnail
- jokes that work from the visual situation

Avoid:

- dense slang
- fast abstract claims
- idioms that carry the whole joke
- cultural references needed to understand the opening

## Required Future Hook Workflow

When a future video reaches hook planning:

1. Read the chosen title-thumbnail pair.
2. Identify the dominant object.
3. Identify the main contradiction.
4. Choose one WIT emotion.
5. Choose one hook type.
6. Fill out [first-10-seconds-board-template.md](hook-templates/first-10-seconds-board-template.md).
7. Score the hook with [hook-scorecard.md](hook-templates/hook-scorecard.md).
8. Do not continue into full production unless the hook passes.

## Testing Method

For a future produced hook:

1. Export only the first `10` seconds.
2. Watch without sound.
3. Watch at `25%` size.
4. Pause at seconds `1`, `3`, `6`, and `9`.
5. Ask whether each paused frame is interesting.
6. Confirm the title-thumbnail promise appears by second `10`.
7. Only continue production if the hook passes the scorecard.

## Hard Rules For Future Openings

A future opening fails if:

- the first board is just a title card
- branding appears before the curiosity event
- the topic is unclear without audio
- the contradiction is invisible without audio
- WIT is neutral or decorative
- the first line sounds like a school presentation
- the hook explains the mechanism before creating suspicion
- the thumbnail promise is missing by second `10`
- the hook cannot be understood on mobile
