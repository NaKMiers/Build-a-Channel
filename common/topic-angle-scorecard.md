# Topic Angle Scorecard

Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Purpose:
score future `Why It Works` topic angles before they become scripts or production projects.

This scorecard is reusable. It does not score any existing video by itself.

## Score Scale

Score each category from `1-5`.

```text
1 = weak / missing
2 = present but unclear
3 = usable with revision
4 = strong
5 = unusually strong
```

Total possible score:

```text
40 points
```

## Production Threshold

```text
34-40: Strong candidate. Ready for rough title, thumbnail tension, and first 10 seconds.
30-33: Usable candidate. Revise weak categories before scripting.
24-29: Not ready for a normal long video. Revise, convert to short-form test, or label as experiment.
Under 24: Reject for now.
```

Hard rule:

```text
Do not produce a normal long video under 30/40 unless the user explicitly approves it as an experiment.
```

Critical category rule:

```text
Curiosity, Visual motif, Explanation depth, and Packaging strength must each score at least 3/5.
```

## Category Guides

### Curiosity

Score high when:

- the angle creates a question immediately
- the contradiction is clear in one sentence
- the viewer can sense there is a hidden mechanism

Score low when:

- the premise sounds like a textbook explainer
- the answer feels obvious from the title
- the topic is useful but not surprising

### Relatability

Score high when:

- the viewer has personally felt the pain
- the problem shows up in daily life
- the topic creates `this happens to me too`

Score low when:

- only a narrow niche understands the problem
- the pain is abstract or distant
- the video depends on caring about industry details first

### Visual Motif

Score high when:

- one object or situation can return across the video
- the motif can explain, joke, and transition
- the motif is easy to read at mobile size

Score low when:

- the video would mostly show text
- every section needs a different unrelated image
- the main idea cannot become a repeated object

### Humor Potential

Score high when:

- WIT can be trapped, billed, tricked, chased, corrected, or quietly defeated by the system
- at least `5` visual jokes are easy to imagine
- the humor supports the explanation

Score low when:

- WIT would only point at labels
- the joke is only a line in the script
- humor would require mean, misleading, or obscure references

### English Learner Fit

Score high when:

- the topic teaches useful real-world vocabulary naturally
- examples are concrete and visible
- the English can stay simple without becoming childish

Score low when:

- the topic needs too much unexplained jargon
- the jokes depend on native-only wordplay
- the video would become a vocabulary lesson instead of an explainer

### Explanation Depth

Score high when:

- there is a real business, social, internet, money, or behavior system behind the pain
- the video can reveal `what people think`, `what is actually happening`, and `why it keeps happening`
- the final insight is more than common advice

Score low when:

- the video is mainly opinion
- the explanation is one obvious sentence
- research would only add filler

### Packaging Strength

Score high when:

- the title can name the hidden logic
- the thumbnail can show the weird situation
- title and thumbnail can avoid repeating the same information
- the first `10` seconds can pay off the package immediately

Score low when:

- the thumbnail would be only words
- the title has to over-explain the concept
- the package needs clickbait to feel interesting

### Production Feasibility

Score high when:

- HyperFrames can show the idea with simple boards
- reusable WIT poses, comedy props, real-life objects, UI mockups, or red markup can carry the visuals
- the angle can be made without unsafe or copied assets

Score low when:

- the video requires expensive animation
- the strongest visuals require copyrighted screenshots or real logos
- the angle depends on footage the channel cannot safely use

## Hard Fails

An angle fails even with a high score if:

- it is misleading
- it relies on rage bait
- it copies another creator's premise, thumbnail, or joke structure too closely
- it cannot be explained honestly in simple English
- it has no real-life object, UI, receipt, phone, product, paper, or physical evidence option
- it has no clear WIT role
- it would push the channel away from money, internet, society, business, or modern life
- it turns the channel into direct Deewas promotion too early
