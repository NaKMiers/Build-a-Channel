# First 10 Seconds Hook Scorecard

Classification: `Core`

Scope: `CHANNEL_WIDE`

Purpose:
decide whether a future `Why It Works` opening is strong enough before full script or production work continues.

This is a reusable gate. It does not score any existing video by itself.

## Pass Threshold

Score out of `100`.

```text
90-100: Strong. Ready for full production planning.
80-89: Usable, but fix the weakest area first.
65-79: Not ready. Rewrite the hook board.
0-64: Reject. Start again from the contradiction.
```

Hard fail rules override the score.

## Score Table

| Area | Points | What To Check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Topic clarity by second 3 | 15 | The viewer can understand the broad topic or situation with no audio. |
| Contradiction by second 5 | 20 | Something feels visibly wrong, unfair, suspicious, or funny before the hook is halfway over. |
| WIT emotional position | 15 | WIT clearly shows how the system feels and is not neutral or decorative. |
| Thumbnail promise by second 10 | 15 | The hook pays off the title-thumbnail promise immediately. |
| Curiosity gap | 15 | The viewer has a clear reason to keep watching after the first 10 seconds. |
| Learner-friendly narration | 10 | The first line is simple, specific, human, and easy to follow. |
| Mobile readability | 10 | The hook still reads at small size with short labels and strong silhouettes. |

Total: `100`

## Detailed Rubric

### 1. Topic Clarity By Second 3 - 15

Score high if:

- one dominant object is visible immediately
- the first label is short and concrete
- the viewer knows the topic world without audio
- the board does not look like a generic title card

Score low if:

- the first image is abstract
- the viewer needs narration to understand the topic
- multiple objects compete for attention
- branding arrives before the situation

### 2. Contradiction By Second 5 - 20

Score high if:

- the normal object becomes suspicious quickly
- a hidden cost, rule, trap, downgrade, or weird result appears
- the contradiction works visually, not only in narration
- the viewer can pause at second `5` and ask a clear question

Score low if:

- the first half only introduces background
- the contradiction is explained but not shown
- the visual is clean, polite, or too informational
- the suspicious detail is too small to notice

### 3. WIT Emotional Position - 15

Score high if:

- WIT has one obvious emotion
- WIT reacts to the object or contradiction
- the emotion matches the thumbnail and topic
- the pose is readable on mobile

Score low if:

- WIT is neutral
- WIT is only a presenter
- WIT looks disconnected from the situation
- the emotion is too subtle or ambiguous

### 4. Thumbnail Promise By Second 10 - 15

Score high if:

- the same object, motif, or contradiction appears in the hook
- the opening proves the thumbnail was not clickbait
- the title's hidden logic begins to feel real
- the viewer understands why the thumbnail looked weird

Score low if:

- the hook starts a different idea from the thumbnail
- the promised object or contradiction appears late
- the hook could belong to a different video
- the opening is less interesting than the package

### 5. Curiosity Gap - 15

Score high if:

- the hook creates one clear unanswered question
- the viewer understands the problem but not the whole mechanism
- the final beat points toward a larger system
- the next section feels necessary

Score low if:

- the hook answers everything
- the hook asks a vague question
- the viewer sees a topic but no tension
- the hook feels like a full summary instead of an opening event

### 6. Learner-Friendly Narration - 10

Score high if:

- the first line uses common words
- sentence length is short
- the joke works from context
- the line sounds spoken, not written
- the contradiction is easy to hear

Score low if:

- the line is abstract
- the line depends on dense idiom or slang
- the first sentence is too long
- the voice sounds like an essay introduction

### 7. Mobile Readability - 10

Score high if:

- text is short and large
- silhouettes are strong
- there is enough empty space
- the paused frames at seconds `1`, `3`, `6`, and `9` are readable

Score low if:

- labels are tiny
- the scene has a complex dashboard
- WIT or the object is too small
- important details only work at full desktop size

## Hard Fails

A hook fails even with a high score if:

- the first board is only a title card
- branding appears before the hook event
- the topic is unclear without audio
- no contradiction is visible by second `5`
- WIT is neutral or decorative
- the first line begins with `In today's video`
- the hook explains before it creates suspicion
- the thumbnail promise is not visible by second `10`
- the scene cannot be understood at mobile size

## Review Checklist

Use this checklist after scoring:

```text
[ ] One dominant object by second 3
[ ] One visible contradiction by second 5
[ ] One clear WIT emotion by second 8
[ ] Title-thumbnail promise paid off by second 10
[ ] First spoken line is simple and specific
[ ] No branding before the curiosity event
[ ] No long on-screen sentence
[ ] No complex dashboard
[ ] Works without sound
[ ] Works at 25% size
[ ] Pause frames at 1, 3, 6, and 9 seconds are interesting
```
