# Quirk log

One row per video. New rows appended at the bottom. The `script` and `scenes`
skills read the last entries before drafting a new episode. A quirk is a small,
intentional, non-formulaic moment that breaks the polished pattern just enough to
mark the script as human-made. Voice quirk lives in `script_<short_slug>.md`;
cast quirk lives in `prompts/visual-plan.md` as a one-word `Delta`. Zero is
acceptable, two is not.
