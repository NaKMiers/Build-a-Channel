The pen is not a separate character. It is the signature prop on the @YOU sheet so it remains visually stable without being given a face or personality that the script never gives it. The participants in the two laboratory studies, the researchers, the audience on the video call, and the people at other modern thresholds are confined to local sections and can remain generic background figures.

The anthropology cast uses a restrained early twentieth-century island fishing interpretation. The garments are plain work wraps translated into the channel's filled-torso doodle construction. They are not ceremonial costumes. Avoid generic caveman imagery, feathers, face paint, bone jewelry, and invented decorative motifs. @FISHER wears brown `#8B5E3C`; @CREW wears sky blue `#6EB5E8` so the individual and group separate instantly against warm sand, wood, and sunlight. @YOU keeps the canonical hoodie `#2E77C4` because the modern bookend does not justify a costume change.
