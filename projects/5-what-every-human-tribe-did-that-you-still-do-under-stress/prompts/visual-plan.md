## Story map

Hook: An already straight pen is nudged before a video call; the desk order builds into a read-touch-check-return loop, the unseen questions remain unchanged, and the powerless half-inch movement visibly releases the body just before the call begins.
Causal chain: A harmless fixed sequence quiets the body -> stress narrows flexible action into ritualization -> Lang shows movement becoming rigid under threat -> Hobson and Inzlicht separate regulation from skill -> Malinowski shows ritual intensifying where practical control ends -> shared ritual coordinates people -> the same mechanism can become an endless certainty loop -> digital life removes visible thresholds -> one fixed final action restores an honest transition into work.
Recurring anchor: MOTIF: the cobalt pen returns as a powerless outcome object, a bodily order switch, a bounded threshold marker, and finally an anchor for attention rather than the future.
Ending convergence: The metal object, smaller error signal, Trobriand canoe preparation, and straight pen merge into uncertainty expanding while behavior contracts; the canoe leaves shore, the call begins, and the pen is reinterpreted as holding attention steady long enough for readiness to become movement.
