## Description

```
Everyone tells you to stay calm, your therapist, your boss, your mother most of all, but your ancestors would call that advice dangerous. Psychologists Lisa Sell, John Tooby, and Leda Cosmides found that anger is not a loss of control, it is a precisely engineered negotiation your brain runs to recalibrate how much someone weighs your welfare. Anthropologist Christopher Boehm traced the deeper layer, the small hunter-gatherer bands where looking weak meant losing your share of the kill and your place in the group. That is why the feeling floods your body before you have decided anything, it was built for stakes that used to be life or death. This video shows you why the signal still fires today even when the audience it was built for is gone. And it shows you the one shift that makes anger work for you instead of against you.
```
