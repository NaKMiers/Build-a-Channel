# Section 4 Marked Script — Why Companies Love It: One Sale Becomes Forever

Marked for delivery only. The `**bold**` joke markers and `[beat]` cues are NOT spoken.
Real pacing/rhythm lives in `tts-inputs/section-04-why-companies-love-it-tts.txt`.

```text
So why did everyone switch to this? Simple. Follow the money. It's always the money.

Imagine you sell a coffee machine. You make one sale, you say thank you, and then you wait — maybe years — hoping that person ever comes back.

Now imagine instead they pay you a little every month. Automatically. Possibly forever. Even on the months they don't touch the thing.

Which business would you rather own? [beat] Yeah. Me too.

A customer who buys once is worth a little. A customer who pays every month is worth a lot — because that payment just keeps coming. Like rain. Or relatives.

This is the magic word behind everything now: recurring. Recurring money is predictable. A company can plan its entire future around your forgetfulness.

So the goal quietly changed. It used to be: make something good enough that you buy it. Now it can also be: make something sticky enough that you never quite leave. Less "great product." More "beautiful trap."
```

## Delivery intent (from 02-script.md voice revision notes)

- `[beat]` after "Which business would you rather own?" (then the dry "Yeah. Me too.").
- Keep "Like rain. Or relatives." light and quick — minimal hold, near-tossed-off.

## Rhythm style applied in the TTS input (user's anti-monotone toolkit)

- Trailing `...` setups before payoffs ("Now imagine instead...", "the magic word behind everything
  now..." → "recurring").
- Stacked `...` hold on "Which business would you rather own?" to land the dry "Yeah. Me too."
- `. .` staccato resets on the punchy beats ("Follow the money. .", "Automatically. .",
  "Recurring money is predictable. .", "Less great product. .").
- The "recurring" magic word is isolated on its own line after a hold so it stands out.
- "Like rain. Or relatives." kept on close adjacent lines with single periods only — quick, not milked.
- Measured behavior reminder: ellipses add real time; `. .` resets prosody without adding length;
  commas/line breaks add no pause; `_` does nothing.
