# Section 5 Marked Script — The Free Trial Is A Countdown

Marked for delivery only. The `**bold**` joke markers and `[beat]/[deadpan]` cues are NOT spoken.
Real pacing/rhythm lives in `tts-inputs/section-05-free-trial-countdown-tts.txt`.

```text
Here's the genius part. To get you in, they make it feel free.

"Start your free trial!" No charge today! Just pop in your card — you know, for no reason at all. [deadpan] Totally normal. Strangers love holding your card.

And the trial is real. It really is free. For seven days. After that, a tiny countdown you can't see hits zero, and "free" quietly becomes a payment.

And most people don't cancel on day seven. They forget. Not because they're careless — because forgetting is the design. The charge is small, the date is fuzzy, life is loud.

So the little payment just... continues. Forever. A ghost, living in your bank account. [beat] Rent free. Well — not rent free. That's the whole problem.

Open your bank statement right now and you'll probably find one. A mystery charge. Three dollars. Every month. For a thing you opened once, in a year you cannot name.

[deadpan] Your free trial of financial awareness has expired.
```

## Delivery intent (from 02-script.md voice revision notes)

- `[deadpan]` on "Strangers love holding your card." and on the closing gag.
- `[beat]` before "Rent free."

## Rhythm style applied in the TTS input (user's anti-monotone toolkit)

- Trailing `...` setups before payoffs ("Just pop in your card...", "So the little payment just...",
  "Your free trial of financial awareness...").
- Stacked `...` holds to land the two deadpan jokes ("Strangers love holding your card." and the
  closing "...has expired.") and the "Rent free." beat.
- `. .` staccato resets on the punchy statement-list beats ("For seven days. .", "A mystery charge. .",
  "Three dollars. .", "Every month. .").
- "continues." and "Forever." split with holds so the ghost-payment idea lands slowly.
- Measured behavior reminder: ellipses add real time; `. .` resets prosody without adding length;
  commas/line breaks add no pause; `_` does nothing.
