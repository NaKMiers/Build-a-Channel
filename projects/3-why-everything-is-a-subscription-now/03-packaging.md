## Packaging Brief

- Core promise: Explain how a subscription looks like a small convenient monthly payment, but is really a quiet shift from owning things to renting your own life.
- Main contradiction: You pay forever but own nothing; subscribing takes 10 seconds, cancelling takes a fight.
- Audience question: Why do I pay for so many monthly things I can't even name - and why is it so hard to stop?
- Recurring motif: every object WIT touches sprouts a little `$/mo` tag; the only tag-free thing is his paycheck.
- WIT emotion: happy at signup -> suspicious as tags spread -> ambushed by a free-trial flip -> trapped at the cancel maze -> quiet, knowing at the payoff.
- First 10 seconds promise: WIT buys something and smiles; a tiny `$4.99/mo` tag pops onto it; more tags pop onto everything around him; "Somewhere along the way, you stopped buying things. You started renting your whole life."
- Risk to avoid: do not turn it generic/preachy ("subscriptions are bad / just cancel them") and do not blame the viewer for signing up; keep it system-explains-it.
