I find a chair
for nine dollars.
Four legs, a seat,
the confidence of
a much better chair.
So he buys it.
For the first week,
everything is fine.
Then it makes a noise
like legal advice.
The screw gets loose.
One leg begins exploring
other career options.
And suddenly, the cheap
chair was not really cheap.
A normal chair with
one small problem...
So next time
something looks cheap,
the better question
isn't "how much?"
