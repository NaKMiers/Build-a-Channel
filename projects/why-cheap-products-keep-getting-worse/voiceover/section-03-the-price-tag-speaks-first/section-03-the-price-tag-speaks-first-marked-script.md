# Section 3 Marked Script

[emphasis: price tag] The first reason is that the price tag speaks first. [pause]

When you buy something, the price is easy to understand.

Nine dollars is nine dollars.

Your wallet can read that very quickly. [pause]

But the future cost is quiet. [beat]

It does not stand next to the product and say, "Hello, I am the replacement you will buy in three months." [pause]

That would be helpful.

[deadpan] Also, very bad marketing. [beat]

So products compete on the things you can see immediately.

Low price.

Fast delivery.

New color.

Extra feature. [pause]

A sale sticker that looks like it has personally saved your life. [beat]

But if the visible price has to stay low, something else may become negotiable. [pause]

[slower] Sometimes that something is tomorrow. [beat]
