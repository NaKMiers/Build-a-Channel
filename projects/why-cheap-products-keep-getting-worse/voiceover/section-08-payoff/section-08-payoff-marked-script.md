# Section 8 Marked Script

So why do cheap products keep getting worse? [pause]

Not because cheap always means bad.

And not because every product is secretly planning to betray you. [beat]

The better answer is that modern products are under pressure to win the price you see today. [pause]

So the future you do not see becomes easier to cut. [beat]

[slower] The real price is not only what you pay at checkout. [pause]

[slower] It is also how long the thing works, how easy it is to fix, and how soon you have to buy it again. [beat]

So the next time something looks like a bargain, the better question is not only:

"How much does this cost?" [pause]

It is:

[slower] "How much future is included?" [beat]
