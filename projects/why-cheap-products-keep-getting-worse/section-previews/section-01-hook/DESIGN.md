# DESIGN

Project:
`Why Cheap Products Keep Getting Worse`

Composition:
`Section01Hook`

## Style Prompt

Photo-first explainer boards with warm real-world texture, one suspicious yellow price tag, dry handwritten labels, and WIT reacting like the buyer who realizes the future cost was hidden under the seat the whole time.

## Colors

- Paper background: `#efe7d9`
- Ink: `#181310`
- Sale yellow: `#efc63d`
- Red markup: `#ba2d2d`
- Warm blue card: `#d7e4ef`
- Shadow brown: `rgba(56, 39, 25, 0.22)`

## Typography

- Handwritten labels: `cursive`
- Utility text and receipt notes: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Motion

- Hard-cut rhythm with very short board-to-board handoffs.
- Every board gets a clear entrance, then mostly stillness.
- Photo bases drift only slightly; labels and markup do the emphasis work.
- Cue-critical words must already be readable on the spoken beat.

## What Not To Do

- Do not make the hook vector-only.
- Do not use heavy global zooms or cinematic sweeps.
- Do not let WIT cover the chair leg, screw, receipt totals, or final stamp.
- Do not add more than one red-markup joke per board.
- Do not depend on baked-in source text from any image.
