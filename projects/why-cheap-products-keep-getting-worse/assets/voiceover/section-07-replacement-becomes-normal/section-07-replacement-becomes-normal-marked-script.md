# Section 7 Marked Script

When repair gets slow, confusing, or expensive, replacement starts looking normal. [pause]

Not because people love throwing things away.

Most people do not wake up and say, "Today I would like to create a small landfill with my headphones." [beat]

They replace things because the system makes replacement feel easier. [pause]

The repair quote is high.

The spare part is missing.

The product is already out of support.

The new one arrives tomorrow. [pause]

So WIT buys the product again. [pause]

The price tag smiles again.

The receipt prints again. [beat]

[deadpan] And the cheap product quietly becomes a subscription with extra steps. [beat]
