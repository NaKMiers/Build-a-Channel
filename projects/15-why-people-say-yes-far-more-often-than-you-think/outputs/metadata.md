## Description

```
You already know who would say no. You have never asked them. There was something you needed this month, a favor, an introduction, twenty minutes of someone's time, and the request never left your head.

That was not shyness. It was a prediction about another person, and researchers have measured how wrong it is. You sit on one side of an ask and the only thing you can see is what the yes would cost them. The person you are afraid to ask is not looking at that number at all. They are looking at what the no would cost, out loud, to your face, and that is the expensive one. In a band of a hundred and fifty people the odds of a yes were never in doubt, so your mind was never issued an estimator. It was issued a brake. This video is about what happened to the road the brake was built for, and the one thing that changes the answer more than who you ask.
```
