## Description

```
The month you have the least money is the month you handle it worst. You miss the payment, you forget the appointment, you buy the thing you cannot afford, and you already have the explanation ready. You are bad with money. Undisciplined. Short-sighted. When researchers actually measured it, that story inverted.

Being short of money does not reveal a worse mind. It produces one, by spending a resource researchers call bandwidth, which is the mental capacity you have left over for everything else. This video shows that capacity measured in the same people twice, a few months apart, and shows you exactly where it went. Then it goes back to a world where a shortage lasted days instead of years and the exit was a person standing about thirty feet away, which is the world your narrowing attention was built for. You walk away with one shift, and it is not a budget.
```
