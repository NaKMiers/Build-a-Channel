## Description

```
By six o'clock, your phone battery is almost dead, and somehow you are too. You did nothing all day, yet your mind feels rubbed raw. That is because doing nothing and resting are not the same thing. Your attention was working the whole time, testing one option after another and finding nowhere to land. Psychologists call this boredom, an ancient alarm that pushes you toward a better use of your time. It evolved for a world with real edges, not an endless feed with no final page. This video shows why the exhaustion was never about laziness, and what rest actually needs instead.
```
