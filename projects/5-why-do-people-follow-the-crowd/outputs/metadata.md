## Description

```
You have clapped for something you did not love, hands moving before your opinion caught up. That is not weakness, it is Solomon Asch's 1951 line experiment playing out in your body. Your brain treats the crowd as a sense organ, reading agreement the way it reads your own eyes. Brain scans by Gregory Berns show conformity changing perception itself, not just what you say out loud. For two hundred thousand years, trusting the group's tested knowledge, like the manioc-processing rules that kept whole villages alive, was survival. Screens and star ratings now trigger that same ancient trust with none of the testing behind them. By the end you will have one simple question that tells you when to follow the crowd and when to trust your own eyes.
```
