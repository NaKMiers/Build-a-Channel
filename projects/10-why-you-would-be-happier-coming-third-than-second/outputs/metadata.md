## Description

```
The athlete who comes second is measurably less happy than the athlete who comes third. Not a feeling, not sometimes. A number, pulled off the faces of Olympic medalists at the moment they finished. You already have an explanation for that, and it is wrong.

Your mind never scores your life against your life. It scores it against an invented one standing right beside it, and the closer that invented world sits, the louder it hurts. This video is about why the near miss aches worse than the far one, why a band of thirty people built that machine into you two hundred thousand years ago, and the one question that gets the invented rival out of the room.
```
