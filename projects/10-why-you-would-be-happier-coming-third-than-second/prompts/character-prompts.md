Derivation notes, written here so `scenes` does not re-litigate them:

- **@SILVER and @BRONZE are both locked, and neither can be a group entry.** The video's entire
  claim is a comparison between two individuals who are two steps apart, so a single podium group
  cannot carry it. Both bookend: they open the video in line 1, carry the whole psychology section,
  and each returns at the end, @BRONZE in the shift at line 37 and @SILVER in the closing echo at
  line 39. Same reasoning that cast two separate friend groups in project 3, where a collision
  needed both halves on screen.
- **@RIVAL is the highest drift exposure on this cast, which is why an abstraction beat a vivid
  one-off for the slot.** It is named in the hook (the invented one standing right beside it, the
  invented rival out of the room), in the mechanism, in the feed at line 31, in the shift at line
  35, in the crossing-out at line 37 and in the final image at line 39. The two travellers who miss
  their flights are more vivid, but they are two contiguous paragraphs, so drift is bounded and
  `scenes` can carry them with generic figures. Ranked by moments times spread, @RIVAL wins.
- **@RIVAL is a personified concept, not a person, and it is never Toss in ghost form.** Toss only
  ever plays @YOU. The unreality is carried by CONSTRUCTION, a fully filled body including the head
  and a broken dashed outer contour, not by removing the face. Asking an image model for a blank or
  featureless head produced a field of giant white eggs in an earlier thumbnail round, so @RIVAL
  keeps a complete simple face drawn in charcoal directly on its fill.
- **The phone is @YOU's signature prop, not a cast entry.** The feed at line 31 causes the effect
  but is never the subject. The subject is the invented person it delivers, which is @RIVAL. Same
  cut as the phone-and-case pair in project 8: cast the one the narration keeps returning to and
  give the other to whoever holds it.
- **The anthropology is Kalahari, not prehistoric.** Line 25 names Richard Lee and the Ju/'hoansi
  and the section is set in a present-day forager camp. Line 19's two hundred thousand years is
  narration framing, not a second setting. There is no caveman in this video.
- **@YOU wears a NEW outfit, as every video now requires.** An open zip-up track jacket in saturated
  orange `#F5820D` over a charcoal `#3F3F46` tee and his charcoal shorts. Derived from the script's
  bookend, where he is at home in the evening with a phone in his hand at line 29 and standing in
  his kitchen at line 37. The open front, the stand collar and the vertical zip line make the
  silhouette genuinely different from the closed crew-necks of the last two videos, so this is a new
  outfit and not a recolour. Orange is worn by no other member of this cast. Keep @YOU off warm
  orange grounds, which here means the campfire: he is never in a camp scene, so the conflict does
  not arise.
- **@SILVER red `#D94040` against @BRONZE grass green `#3A9E3A` is the thesis in two colours.** The
  ache and the relief, readable in one glance in a split frame. It is also why @YOU could not be
  coral: coral `#D96F5F` and red `#D94040` are the same family and the two share frames around line
  33 and line 37.
- **@RIVAL carries the lavender chapter colour on its body, which is a deliberate exception.** It is
  the embodiment of that chapter rather than a figure standing inside it. So @RIVAL never stands on
  a lavender ground: every frame containing it sits on cream `#FFF4DE` or a tinted card of another
  chapter, and lavender cards are reserved for the @RIVAL-absent diagram and card beats. Same shape
  as the documented group-blue exception shipped in projects 2, 3 and 8, written down once here.
- **@CAMP takes sky blue `#6EB5E8` for the cool read against its own warm tan ground**, the default
  established over three previous videos, while @HUNTER keeps ancestral brown `#8B5E3C` so the
  ancestral colour family still reads in a split frame. Sky blue `#6EB5E8` is not saturated channel
  blue `#2D5FBF`, which stays reserved.
- **No blue exception is needed for Toss this time.** He is orange, so @CAMP's sky blue is the only
  blue on the sheet.
- **The kill stays non-graphic.** Line 25 is the video's centrepiece anthropology visual and it is
  about a dead animal. @HUNTER's prop is a carrying pole with an abstract wrapped bundle, explicitly
  negated for animal, fur, head, legs and blood. The mockery reads from the camp's faces and
  gestures, which is where the script puts it anyway.
- **Both medals are text traps and trademark traps.** A medal disc invites an engraved number, a
  year, and five interlocking rings. Every medal panel on every sheet carries enumerated negatives
  for all of it.
