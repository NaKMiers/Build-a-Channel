## Description

```
You ordered the salad. And then you ordered the cake. Not because you forgot the salad, but because you remembered it, and your brain decided the first choice had purchased the right to dessert.

That is not a failure of willpower or a lapse in discipline. It is a psychological mechanism called moral licensing, running an ancient internal ledger where every deposit quietly authorizes a withdrawal. For hundreds of thousands of years, that ledger was public, tracked by forty watchful eyes around a campfire who decided what you had earned and when you had taken too much. When that audience vanished, the machinery moved indoors without an auditor, and your mind has been issuing itself unearned receipts ever since.
```
