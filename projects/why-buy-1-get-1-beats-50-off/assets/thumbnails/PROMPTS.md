# Thumbnail Prompts — Why Buy 1 Get 1 Free Beats 50% Off

Ready-to-paste prompts for generating the 5 A/B thumbnails in ChatGPT / DALL·E.
Source of record (scores + decisions) is `../../03-packaging.md`. These are the copy-paste blocks.

**Intensity: MAX shock / clickbait / outrage / curiosity.** Cranked hard per owner request.
Honesty line kept (channel guardrail / skill hard-fail): the NUMBERS stay real ($5, $10, $1, $2 from
the research) and the hooks are curiosity QUESTIONS (`A TRICK?!`, `ROBBED?!`, `$10?!`), not lies. No
"ONLY TODAY" fake urgency, no fake stat, no hateful targeting of a real brand/person. It should feel
like rage-bait — without being a lie.

## How To Use

1. Open ChatGPT (image generation) or DALL·E.
2. **Attach the WIT reference image:**
  `.agents/_shared/assets/wit/poses/wit-pose-neutral-front.png`
   (so the character stays on-model — Claude can't generate images, you do.)
3. For **Variant A** and **Variant B** (the split-screen comparisons), ALSO attach the approved
  comparison layout reference:
   `projects/why-cheap-products-keep-getting-worse/assets/thumbnails/variant-c-generated.png`
   so the divider / corner-tag / center-hook layout matches the approved comparison style.
4. Paste ONE variant block at a time (each block is self-contained).
5. Ask for **16:9, 1280×720**.
6. Save the result as the named file (`variant-a-generated.png` … `variant-e-generated.png`)
  into this folder.
7. **Re-roll line if WIT drifts:** "Keep the character EXACTLY like the attached reference —
  round white head, thick black outline, oversized black glasses, no hair, no shirt, no tie,
   no shoes. Only change his pose and expression."
8. **Reject rule:** if the result gives WIT hair, a shirt/tie, or shoes, it is off-model — reject
  and re-roll. WIT is a bare white round-headed glasses figure only.

**A/B generate-first order:** D → A → B → C → E. (D is the loudest single-face shock; A/B are the
two curiosity comparisons; C and E are the big dramatic swings.)
