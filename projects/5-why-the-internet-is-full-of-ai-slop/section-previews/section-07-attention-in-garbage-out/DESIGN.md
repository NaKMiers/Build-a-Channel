# Section 7 Design - Payoff: Attention In, Garbage Out

- Composition: `Section07Payoff`, 1920x1080, 43.413s.
- Big idea: the **payoff** - the section where the whole video's thesis lands. Recap the question
  (WHY?), give the one-line answer (not because AI is evil, not a conspiracy - the internet is working
  exactly as built: it pays for attention, slop grabs attention, so slop wins), compress it to the
  title beat (ATTENTION IN / GARBAGE OUT), reframe (the internet is not broken, it is WORKING PERFECTLY
  - that is the problem), hand over the practical upgrade (you now know THE TELLS), then empower and
  sign off (you can't stop the flood but you can stop being fooled; the person who pays attention on
  purpose wins; keep your eyes open).
- 7 connected big scenes, one per beat, each on its own track with a short crossfade. Hard cuts +
  hard-show by default; impact motion (smash/pop) reserved for WHY?, the two struck claims + "slop
  wins.", ATTENTION IN / GARBAGE OUT (opposite slides), BROKEN / WORKING PERFECTLY, THE TELLS header +
  its three rows, FOOLED / "you win", and the closing "keep your eyes open."
- Through-line motif: the **slop engine** recurs (7.1 / 7.3 / 7.4 / 7.7) as the machine the whole
  channel keeps "explaining"; the **slop-wins trophy podium** is the literal answer image for 7.2 (slop
  on the top step holding the cup, the honest creator slumped on the low step).
- Bases graded by mood: dark machine hall (the system, 7.1/7.3/7.4), dark spotlight stage (the contest
  slop wins, 7.2), evidence desk (the tells, 7.5), bright calm window (the empowerment + sign-off,
  7.6/7.7) - the deliberate dark-to-bright lift across the section mirrors flood -> clarity.
- Mascot: one giant WIT per scene (~1/3-1/2 frame, high anchor, legs-only crop), pose + side varied to
  match the arc (curious question -> open-palm explaining -> raised-finger lecturing -> deadpan -> open
  presenting -> proud + sure -> direct accusing address to the viewer).
- Type: PatrickHand handwritten labels + Segoe UI black for the kinetic words, struck claim cards, the
  ATTENTION chip, the green WORKING PERFECTLY check, and THE TELLS header.
- Colors: `--gold` for WHY? / slop wins / ATTENTION IN / FOOLED / "you win" / the closing line and the
  TELLS header; `--red` (`#ff5a4d`) for GARBAGE OUT and the strike-through bars; `--green` for the
  WORKING PERFECTLY check and the three tell checkmarks; `--cream` for the calm handwritten lines.
- The TELLS checklist reuses two real AI-tell thumbnails (extra-fingers hand, too-perfect influencer)
  plus one CSS-drawn "headline" tile for the too-good-to-be-true story, each with a green check - a
  tidy, scannable payoff card.
- Safety: AI is never called evil (that frame is literally struck through); the edge stays on the
  incentive ("it pays for attention", "WORKING PERFECTLY - that is the problem"); no real person or
  company is named or depicted; the trophy podium uses the abstract slop mascot, not a real creator.
