# Section 5 Design - It Already Got Out

- Composition: `Section05GotOut`, 1920x1080, 53.739s.
- Big idea: the slop did not stay in "funny Facebook pictures" - it **got out** into every domain of
  life. The section is a rising tour through music, books, kids' content, and work, each a separate
  big scene so an A2-C1 learner follows the list by ear, while the screen literally fills with sludge.
- 7 connected big scenes, one per domain beat, each on its own track with a short crossfade. Hard cuts
  + hard-show by default; impact motion (smash/pop/stamp) only on "it got out.", the "1,000,000
  LISTENERS" card + "0 REAL MEMBERS", "KILL you.", "40%" + the SLOP stamp barrage, "6 LEGS?!",
  "WORKSLOPPED" + "~2 HOURS", and the "congratulations." payoff.
- Continuity device: **THE GREY-SLUDGE FLOOD** (`grey-sludge-flood-1.jpg` callback from S1/S2). A
  `.flood` waterline rises scene by scene (13 / 19 / 27 / 35 / 43 / 53 / 66 %) so the slop visibly
  climbs as each new domain is named, then peaks at 5.7 with WIT drowning while holding one real photo
  above the water.
- Bases: 4 fresh real photos (concert hall / forest floor / living room + TV / office desk) + 2
  deliberate reuse bases (S1 living-room scroll for 5.1; S1/S2 sludge flood for 5.7). Living-room-TV
  is reused across 5.4/5.5 (same room, different kid content). Graded bright/glitz/forest/cool with a
  side scrim on the text half and a light edge vignette - never a full dark wash.
- Mascot: one giant WIT per scene (~1/3-1/2 frame, high anchor, legs-only crop), pose + side varied to
  match the arc (worried -> skeptical -> panic -> disgusted -> cringe -> exhausted -> drowning). 5.7
  centers WIT underwater in goggles + cap as the flood peaks.
- Type: PatrickHand handwritten labels + Segoe UI bold for domain chips / kinetic words / stamps.
- Colors: `--gold #ffd23f` (domain chips, "40%", domain icons), `--green` for the listeners count,
  `--red` for danger (skull, "KILL you.", SLOP stamps, "WORKSLOPPED", the clock chip).
- Safety: AI band = non-existent people (uncanny, no real artist); mushroom guide / horse / document
  are generated parody props with no readable real text or brand; the incidental Apple logo on the
  5.6 desk base is covered by the workslop-document hero.
