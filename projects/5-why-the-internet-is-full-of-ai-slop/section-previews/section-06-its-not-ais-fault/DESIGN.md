# Section 6 Design - It's Not AI's Fault (And Not A Plot)

- Composition: `Section06NotFault`, 1920x1080, 38.933s.
- Big idea: the **honest turn**. After five sections of savage flood, WIT calms down and is FAIR:
  (1) not all AI is slop (a doctor's scan, an artist's tool); slop is a recipe (low effort x high
  volume), not the tool; (2) the "dead internet theory" conspiracy is NAMED and then PUNCTURED ("it's
  dumber than that"); (3) the real cause is no villain - the money rewards it. Dry closer: "you cannot
  arrest an incentive."
- 7 connected big scenes, one per beat, each on its own track with a short crossfade. Hard cuts +
  hard-show by default; impact motion (smash/pop) only on "fair", the two `NOT SLOP` checks, the
  formula, the conspiracy title, the giant red X, NOBODY IS IN CHARGE / NO VILLAIN / $, and the payoff
  lines.
- Tone shift: this is the calmest section, so the grades are bright-but-neutral (no heavy dark wash),
  and the "argument" is carried by clean CSS graphics rather than loud kinetic devices - a level
  balance scale (fairness), green `NOT SLOP` checks, a `SLOP = LOW EFFORT x HIGH VOLUME` formula card.
- Continuity device: the **S2 conspiracy corkboard** returns for the dead-internet-theory beat (6.4)
  and is then crossed out with a giant red X (6.5) - the "name it, then puncture it" move.
- Bases: 4 fresh real photos (courtroom / clinic scan room / workbench / empty boardroom) + 2
  deliberate reuse bases (corkboard from S2, dark spotlight stage from S4.5). Side scrim on the text
  half, light edge vignette - never a full dark wash.
- Mascot: one giant WIT per scene (~1/3-1/2 frame, high anchor, legs-only crop), pose + side varied to
  match the arc (careful pondering -> calm doctor -> making-the-point -> skeptical -> deadpan -> shrug
  -> calm and sure).
- Type: PatrickHand handwritten labels + Segoe UI bold for the formula / conspiracy title / kinetic
  payoff words.
- Colors: `--green` for the NOT SLOP checks, `--gold` for the balance scale / formula / glowing $ /
  payoff, `--red` for the conspiracy title + giant X, `--blue` for the AI-helper badge.
- Safety: AI is shown as a genuine helper (doctor scan, artist tool) so it isn't anti-AI; the
  conspiracy is the abstract "dead internet theory" with an empty tinfoil hat - no real person or
  government official is depicted; the villain throne is deliberately EMPTY (nobody is in charge); the
  edge stays on the incentive (a gold $), never a person or group.
