So - why is the internet full of garbage now?

Not because AI is evil. Not because of a conspiracy. Because the internet is working exactly the way it was built to work. It pays for attention. Slop is very good at grabbing attention. So slop wins.

Attention in. Garbage out.

The internet is not broken. It is doing its job perfectly. That is the problem.

But here is the small, useful upgrade. Now you know the tells. The six fingers. The photo that is a little too perfect. The story that is a little too good to be true.

You cannot stop the flood. But you can stop being fooled by it. And on an internet built on attention, the person who pays attention on purpose is the one who wins.
