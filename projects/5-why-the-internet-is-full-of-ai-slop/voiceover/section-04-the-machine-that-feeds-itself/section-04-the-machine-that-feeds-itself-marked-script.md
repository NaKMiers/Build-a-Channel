Now the important part. Why does the flood keep coming? Follow the money - slowly, because this is the whole video.

Step one. Making content used to be expensive. You needed a writer, a camera, a musician, time. Now you need a prompt and ten seconds. The cost dropped to almost zero.

Step two. The platform does not pay you for being good. It pays you for engagement - and engagement just means clicks, likes, and time spent. [slower] It rewards attention, not quality.

Step three. So the smart move is not to make one great thing. The smart move is to flood the zone - post a thousand cheap things, and hope a few go viral.

Step four. The algorithm that decides what you see? It cannot really tell slop from real. So it serves the slop anyway.

Step five. The slop makes money - which pays for more slop. [beat] The machine feeds itself.

That is the whole engine. Cheap to make, paid by attention, impossible to filter. Of course it floods.
