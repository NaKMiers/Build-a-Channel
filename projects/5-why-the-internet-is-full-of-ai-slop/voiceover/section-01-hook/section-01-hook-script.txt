Quick question. Pick up your phone, open any feed, and scroll for ten seconds. Now be honest: how much of that was actually made by a human?

Three years ago, the answer was easy. All of it.

Today? Good luck.

Because somewhere in that feed there is a photo of a shrimp shaped like Jesus, a news story about an event that never happened, and a hit song by a band that does not exist.

The internet is filling up with garbage. Cheap, fake, mass-produced garbage. And the strange part is, nobody told it to.

Let's find out why.
