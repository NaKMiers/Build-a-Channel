And it is not just funny Facebook pictures anymore. It got out.

Music. A band called the Velvet Sundown passed one million listeners on Spotify before people noticed that not a single member of the band was real.

Books. People are selling AI mushroom-picking guides with fake photos. Pick the wrong mushroom because a robot drew it, and [beat] that one can actually kill you.

Even your job. There is now a word for the co-worker who sends you a beautiful AI document that says absolutely nothing. You just got "workslopped." On average, it costs the person who receives it about two hours to clean up.
