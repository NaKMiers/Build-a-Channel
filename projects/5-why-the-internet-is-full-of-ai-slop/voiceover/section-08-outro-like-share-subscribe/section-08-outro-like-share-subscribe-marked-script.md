# Section 8 Voiceover - Marked Script

Section: `Section 8: Outro: Like, Share, Subscribe`

Markup is for human/timing reference only. HyperFrames Kokoro strips tags; real pacing lives in
`tts-inputs/section-08-outro-like-share-subscribe-tts.txt` (periods + ellipses + `. .`).

```text
That's it for today.

If this helped, like the video, share it with one person who needs it, and subscribe for more. [beat]

See you in the next one.
```

Delivery notes:
- Short, straightforward CTA - calm and direct, not begging.
- `. .` between the three asks (like / share / subscribe) so each lands clearly instead of blurring.
- `[beat]` (a `...` hold) before "See you in the next one."
- Light holds only; this is a quick wind-down after the S7 payoff, not a second climax.
