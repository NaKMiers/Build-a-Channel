```text
So what actually counts as slop? Three things.

One: it looks fine for half a second. Quick glance, looks normal. Look closer, and it falls apart. The hand has six fingers. The words are gibberish. A giant brand spends millions on a holiday ad and misspells its own name as "Coca-Coola." [beat] That really happened.

Two: it costs the maker almost nothing, and it costs you everything. Seconds to make. But it eats your time, your attention, and your trust.

Three: it is made by the thousand. Not one fake post - ten thousand fake posts, sprayed into the feed like a fire hose.

Looks fine, costs them nothing, made by the thousand. [emphasis] That is slop.

A machine pretending to be a person, at full volume.
```

  "That is slop" is the stamp.
- Final line slows for the motif ("a machine pretending to be a person").
