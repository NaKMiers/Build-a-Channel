## Raw Idea

Your feed is suddenly full of weird AI pictures, fake products, and articles that say
nothing. The internet did not get worse by accident. AI made content almost free to
produce, so platforms now reward whoever floods the most, and the cheap "slop" wins because
the algorithm cannot tell garbage from good.

## Why Slop Wins (mechanism to ground research, keep honest)

The script must explain the real incentive chain, not just complain:

1. AI dropped the cost of making content to near zero (text, images, video, voices).
2. Platforms pay attention, not quality. The feed ranks engagement, not truth or effort.
3. So the rational move is to FLOOD: post huge volumes of cheap content and let a few hits pay.
4. Recommendation systems cannot reliably tell slop from real, so slop gets distributed anyway.
5. Ad money and "creator payouts" reward the volume, which funds more slop. The loop tightens.

Must stay honest (honesty guardrail, inherited by research + script):

- Not all AI content is slop. Slop = low-effort, mass-produced, low-value content (often AI),
  made to farm attention, not to be useful. Define it that cleanly on screen.
- "AI" is not the villain on its own; the incentive (pay-for-attention + near-zero cost) is.
  Say this, or the video becomes lazy AI-panic instead of a real "why."
- Cite real, checkable proof in research (e.g. the platform-share stats, named slop genres),
  never invented percentages or made-up view counts.
- Public-figure AI images (e.g. political AI "slop drops") are referenced as a phenomenon only,
  via WIT caricature, never by republishing the real image for mockery.
