Ten people liked it. One did not. Guess which one you took home. The nine you can barely remember, and the one you can quote word for word, in the voice it was said in.

That count is not a character flaw. It is a measurement, and it has a name and a number. Your mind runs a permanent weighting system in which one hostile voice does not weigh the same as one kind voice, and the exchange rate is nowhere near even. This video shows you what that rate actually is, who set it and why they were right to, and the single question that decides whether a stranger gets your evening or not. You are not too sensitive. You are running an alarm that was built for a world where every voice belonged to somebody who had to feed you in winter.
