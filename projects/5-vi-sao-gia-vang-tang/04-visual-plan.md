## Video-Level Direction

- Audience: Vietnamese-native entertainment explainer (Vui Vẻ / Threads-City style); NOT English-learner.
- Renderer: HyperFrames (composited from pre-made isolated assets via `visual-implement`).
- Visual grammar: real / real-looking photo base + mascot drawn on top; **new scene ~every sentence**; vary everything.
- Mascot: WIT (white round head, thick black outline, oversized black glasses), BIG & HIGH, the soul of each scene.
- On-screen tone: deadpan + cheeky at FOMO / the viewer's own wallet; never mock buyers; no investment advice.
- Recurring motif: a gold bar = a fear barometer; "gold earns you nothing".
- Pose library: `analysis/vuive poses/` is a temporary TEST placeholder palette; new poses may be invented and are generated by `visual-implement` (attach the mascot neutral pose).

### Section 1: Hook - the metal that does nothing
Timing basis: `estimated` (no word-timings file; proportional to 29.35s audio).

#### Scene 1.1 - "Vàng không trả lãi. Không trả cổ tức. Không nhắn tin chúc bạn ngủ ngon." (`0:00–0:07`)
- **Role:** opening = "gold is useless" running gag; deadpan at second 0.
- **Layout:** white bg + gray horizon ~88%. Left-center (10–55%): lone gold bar on a clean surface, float+shadow. Right (62–100%, cropped at hip): mascot turned left, palm presenting. Three struck-through labels stacked, one per beat.
- **Mascot:** `pose_deadpan_unimpressed_half_lidded` - right, ~1/3 frame, looking at the bar.
- **On-screen text:** `"không trả lãi"` → `"không trả cổ tức"` → `"không nhắn tin chúc ngủ ngon"` (red strike-through; one per beat; last pops).
- **Emotion/insight:** dry mockery; listing what gold does NOT do.
- **Show-as-you-say:** 3 beats ↔ 3 labels. **Sound/color:** "tick" per label, then silence; gold+red on white.
- **Assets:** `gold-bar-on-table.jpg` (browse-real-photo, left-center, *new*); `pose_deadpan_unimpressed_half_lidded.png` (pose, right, *reuse-library*).

#### Scene 1.2 - "Vậy mà cứ vài tháng, cả nước lại lao đi mua nó như sắp hết hàng." (`0:07–0:12`)
- **Role:** contrast flip - useless, yet everyone rushes to buy.
- **Layout:** full-frame real-looking photo of a crowded gold shop (faces away, no-face). Mascot rises bottom-center, swept in.
- **Mascot:** `pose_excited_giddy_fists_at_face` - bottom-center, ~1/2 frame.
- **On-screen text:** `"cả nước lao đi mua"` + crowd arrow; `"sắp hết hàng?!"`.
- **Show-as-you-say:** "lao đi mua" → mascot pop; "sắp hết hàng" → tag impact. **Sound/color:** crowd murmur ducked; warm bg, bright mascot.
- **Assets:** `gold-shop-crowd.png` (generate-photoreal, background, *new*); `pose_excited_giddy_fists_at_face.png` (pose, *reuse-library*).

#### Scene 1.3 - "…'nơi trú ẩn an toàn' - rồi giành nhau mua đúng lúc nó đắt nhất." (`0:12–0:21`)
- **Role:** the core paradox - "safe" yet buying at the peak.
- **Layout:** background = red gold-price line climbing to a peak; on the PEAK, mascot hugs a gold bar with gold-bar eyes. Ironic `"an toàn?"` label + arrow into the peak.
- **Mascot:** **NEW pose** `pose_hugging_gold_bar_eyes_gold` - hugging gold, gold-bar eyes, greedy grin; ~1/2 frame, on the peak.
- **On-screen text:** `"nơi trú ẩn an toàn"` (quoted) → `"an toàn?"` (red) + arrow to peak.
- **Show-as-you-say:** "nơi trú ẩn an toàn" → quoted label; "đúng lúc đắt nhất" → mascot pops onto peak + "an toàn?" impact. **Sound/color:** metallic "ting"; red line on light field, gold focal at peak.
- **Assets:** `gold-price-chart-rising.png` (generate, background, *new*); `pose_hugging_gold_bar_eyes_gold.png` (**generate - NEW pose**, on peak, *new*).

#### Scene 1.4 - "Vì sao một cục kim loại chẳng làm gì lại tăng điên cuồng? Sao não bạn cứ muốn mua khi giá càng cao?" (`0:21–0:27`)
- **Role:** pose the two core questions (open the curiosity loop).
- **Layout:** mascot CENTER, confused; left: gold bar (REUSED) wearing a big `?`; right: gold-glowing-brain doodle. Two questions appear in turn.
- **Mascot:** `pose_pondering_skeptical_hand_on_chin` - center, ~1/3–1/2 frame.

- **Show-as-you-say:** Q1 ↔ gold bar+"?"; Q2 ↔ brain lights up. **Sound/color:** "?" bounce, "ping"; clean bg + gold glow.
- **Assets:** `gold-bar-on-table.jpg` (**reuse Scene 1.1**, left); `gold-glowing-brain.png` (generate doodle, right, *new*); `pose_pondering_skeptical_hand_on_chin.png` (pose, *reuse-library*).
