## Description

```
Change one word inside a single question, and people will remember broken glass that was never there. Not guess at it. Describe it, defend it, and swear to it a week later. And the reason is not that they were suggestible witnesses. It is that nobody has a recording in their head to check.

Your memory is not playback. It is reconstruction, rebuilt from scratch every single time you reach for it, absorbing whatever it has been told since the event closed. That is why confidence tracks rehearsal rather than accuracy, and why the memory you can describe most vividly is your most edited one. This video is about where that strange design came from, why it kept your ancestors alive in a world with no writing at all, who is quietly feeding it now, and the one question that tells you when to trust yourself.
```
