# Section 1 - Design Notes

Video: `4 Reasons You Can't Get Your First Job Anymore`

Section: `Section 1: Hook: The Ladder Is A Circle` (`19.226s`, 6 scenes)

## Visual Grammar

- P6 standing template: bright real or real-looking photo base per scene, pre-made isolated props, giant WIT, sparse handwritten labels, and hard cuts pinned to the real voice timing.
- Scene rotation: graduation tableau -> job UI -> cork-board requirement -> access-control door -> surreal ladder gag -> clean promise typography.
- Hero motif: `career-ladder-rung-blank.png` is born as a normal ladder on `ladder@13.62` and bends into a closed circle by `circle@14.60`. Scene 1.6 deliberately stops adding to the motif and resets to the real stairwell texture.
- Secondary motif is deferred. Scene 1.4 now explains the paradox through one access-control mechanism: a credential pass and its impossible destination door.
- Motion hierarchy: ordinary labels hard-show; impact motion is reserved for `GRADUATED`, `GOOD.`, and `CIRCULAR EDITION`. The remade promise frame uses hard-shows only.
- WIT arc: proud graduate left -> panic right -> signature deadpan center. Scene 1.6 has no WIT so the final promise can breathe.
- No extra sound assets were created. This preview uses the approved Alan voiceover only.
