The verdict is not that you are broken.

These four reasons do not prove that you are lazy or bad at applying.

The first rung changed.

Some listings are not live opportunities right now. AI may reshape the small tasks that used to train beginners. The experience bar has risen in parts of the market. And slow hiring leaves fewer doors moving.

Those are different problems. A prettier resume cannot solve all four.

Sometimes the useful move is to leave a weak listing faster. Sometimes it is to show direct proof of one skill. And sometimes the honest answer is that the timing and budget are outside your control.

That is frustrating. It is also better than blaming yourself for every unanswered application.

The career ladder may be ridiculous.

But before you climb harder, check whether the first rung is real.

You probably know someone still blaming themselves for this ladder.

They do not need another motivational quote.
