Here is how the loop can work.

When hiring is weak, an employer may feel less pressure to choose quickly.

When employers can wait, they can choose someone who already knows the work.

Beginners find fewer jobs that truly fit them, so they often apply to more listings.

Recruiters receive more applications, so each applicant may receive less useful feedback.

The applicant may see silence and assume the problem is personal.

Then the next employer asks for experience again.

No secret meeting is needed. Each company can make a reasonable choice. Together, those choices can create a ridiculous result. Very efficient. No meeting required.

That is why the experience receipt matters. It can reduce perceived uncertainty for an employer. But when many employers ask for that receipt, beginners struggle to get one.

You cannot control a hiring budget. You cannot force a paused role to wake up.

So before you send the next application, test the rung.
