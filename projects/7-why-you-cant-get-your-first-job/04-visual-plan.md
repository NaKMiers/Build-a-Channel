## Video-Level Direction

- Audience: `A2-C1 English learners (interesting-English advantage)`
- Renderer: `HyperFrames (composited from pre-made assets)`
- Visual grammar: `bright real / real-looking people-free base + mascot or one clear object mechanism; new scene per spoken beat; no two consecutive scenes share a type`
- Mascot character: `WIT - round white head, oversized rectangular glasses, expressive eyebrows; giant and high when present, with role color added through props or costume; emotion stays readable at phone size`
- Tone on screen: `dry, cheeky, and protective of the applicant; edge aimed at hiring incentives and impossible requirements, never applicants, recruiters as people, or any demographic group`
- Primary recurring motif: `the modular career rung. It is born in Section 1 on ladder@13.62, where copies assemble into a ladder and close into a circle on circle@14.60. The same text-free rung returns as transparent in S2, partly removed in S3, raised in S4, trapped behind the slow door in S5, tied to the risk-receipt loop in S6, tested and supplemented in S7, checked in S8, and tagged NO PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE REQUIRED in S9.`
- Secondary recurring motif: `the experience receipt. It is born visually on experience@7.96 in Scene 1.4, becomes the employer's risk receipt in S4-S6, and becomes one clue rather than a verdict in S7-S8.`
- Running state gag: `each system adds a dry corporate patch to the same rung: CIRCULAR EDITION -> VISIBLE / NOT LIVE -> PRACTICE REMOVED -> 5+ YEARS -> ENERGY-SAVING MODE -> RECEIPT REQUIRED -> TESTED -> REAL? -> NO PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE REQUIRED.`
- Mascot emotional arc: `S1 eager graduate -> fooled and deadpan; S2 suspicious ghost hunter; S3 alarmed beginner; S4 frustrated climber; S5 exhausted outsider; S6 recognizes the loop; S7 calm investigator; S8 grounded climber; S9 warm accomplice.`
- Scene-type rotation in use: `lived-in object tableau / animated generic UI / paper hero close-up / circular mechanism / surreal wide gag / promise map / evidence board / checklist device / real-UI process / mascot focus on a real textured base`
- Base treatment: `fresh full-HD base for every scene, bright enough to remain visible; no heavy full-frame dark scrim; local text backing only where needed`
- Sound spine: `paper clicks mark every rung or receipt state change; system sounds cut to silence for the deadpan ladder-circle punchline; the same quiet paper click can return whenever the rung mutates later`
- Safety spine: `all employers, listings, interfaces, and messages are fictional and generic; no copied private screenshot, real applicant data, public-figure target, or employer accusation; AI remains a neutral task tool; every statistic stays scoped in later sections; generated elements remain isolated and text-free so HyperFrames adds exact wording.`
- Pose library: `.agents/_shared/assets/wit/poses/` (palette; Section 1 invents one graduate pose)

### Section 1: Hook: The Ladder Is A Circle

## Section Overview

- Section goal: put the viewer inside the first-job contradiction immediately, turn the job-experience paradox into one visible loop, give birth to the career-rung motif, and promise four reasons plus a practical way in without revealing any answer.
- Duration: `19.226s` (Alan audio `section-01-the-ladder-is-a-circle-alan-elevenlabs.mp3`)
- Timing source: `voiceover/section-01-the-ladder-is-a-circle/section-01-word-timings.json` (Whisper tiny English, generated 2026-07-20; 51 tokens, monotonic; final word end corrected to the Scribe-verified `18.979s`; root duration remains the real `19.226s`).
- Known upstream timing note: the four-reason promise starts at about `15.84s` (`15.859s` in Scribe), later than the channel's first-10-second target. This visual plan preserves the approved current audio and front-loads the topic at `entry@2.02` plus the contradiction at `Three@4.96`; changing promise placement requires a script and voiceover rerun.
- Scene count: `6` (one scene per sentence or paired mirror beat; visible change every `1.36-4.72s`)
- Scene-type rotation: 1.1 lived-in object tableau -> 1.2 animated generic UI -> 1.3 paper hero close-up -> 1.4 circular mechanism -> 1.5 surreal wide gag -> 1.6 promise map.
- Mascot arc in this section: proud new graduate -> no-WIT optimism beat -> panic at the requirement -> no-WIT logic trap -> giant signature deadpan inside the circle -> calm presenter offering a route forward.
- WIT density: `4` appearances across `6` scenes. Scenes 1.2 and 1.4 deliberately let UI and props carry the idea so WIT does not become a reaction sticker on every cue.

### Scene 1.4 - "You need experience for the job. You need the job for experience."

- **Local time:** `7.62-12.34` (first You@7.62, experience@7.96, job@8.88, second You@9.54, job@10.18, experience@10.72)

Two thick hand-drawn CSS/SVG arrows occupy the upper and lower arcs between them, forming a clockwise loop while leaving the center open. No mascot appears; the two objects are large enough to be the characters.
- **Elements:**
  - *Base (full frame):* bright empty office reception or lobby, people-free and brand-free, with visible floor texture and a natural circular or curved composition. It grounds the abstract loop in a real workplace.
  - *Experience receipt (left):* a clean cream receipt with faint generic line marks, no baked words. CSS overlays the single word `EXPERIENCE` and one empty check box.
  - *Job card (right):* same fictional listing card, now simplified to one CSS label: `JOB`.
  - *Loop arrows:* top arrow travels from the receipt to the job card; bottom arrow returns from the job card to the receipt. Their tips are rough red marker shapes, not polished infographic arrows.
- **Mascot:** none by design. This is the second and final no-WIT breathing beat; the moving documents carry the paradox without another reaction shot.

- **Emotion:** trapped logic, clean enough to understand in one glance.
- **Insight / joke:** each prerequisite manufactures the other prerequisite, so the application process has built a machine that produces no starting point.
- **Linkage / eye path:** the first sentence drives left-to-right across the upper arrow; the second sentence drives right-to-left across the lower arrow. When both arrows are complete, the eye is forced into the same circle as the narration.

- **Sound:** one paper tick for the receipt, one for the job card, then two quiet marker strokes. A subtle loop motor starts only after both arrows close and stops instantly at the next scene.
- **Color / contrast:** real pale office neutrals, cream receipt, charcoal card, and one red loop. No heavy overlay; each paper object carries its own shadow and contrast.
