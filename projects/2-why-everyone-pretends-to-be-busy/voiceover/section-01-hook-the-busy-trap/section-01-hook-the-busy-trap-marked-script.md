# Section 1 Marked Script - Hook: The Busy Trap

Video: `Why Everyone Pretends To Be Busy`

Markup is for human voice direction only. It is stripped before TTS (hyperframes tts does not
consume these tags). Keep markup light per `script-learner-voice.md`.

```text
Here is a strange rule about modern life. [pause]

The less time you have for real work, the more important you look. [beat]

A full calendar, a loud inbox, a phone making tiny panic sounds. People see that and think, this person matters.

But sit quietly and think about one hard problem, and people assume you are lazy. [beat] Or asleep with your eyes open.

So everyone gets busy. Or at least, everyone gets very good at looking busy. [beat] [deadpan] There is a difference.
```

Direction notes:
- `[pause]` after the opening line lets the "strange rule" hook land before the payoff.
- `[beat]` before "Or asleep with your eyes open" sets up the dry joke.
- `[beat] [deadpan]` before "There is a difference." is the section-ending dry button (from the script's voice revision note).
- Keep delivery calm and lightly dry - a person explaining something ridiculous without acting surprised.
