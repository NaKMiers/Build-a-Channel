The second reason is that our tools invented emergencies.

Email was supposed to make talking easier. Then chat apps made email faster. Then task apps organized the chat about the email. Then calendar apps booked meetings about why the task app is confusing.

So now the workday is basically five apps interrupting each other.

Everything has a red dot. Everything has a number. Everything says urgent. Because apparently "please ignore this until next week" is bad design.

So your brain starts treating every little notification like a real emergency. A message lands, you reply. Another lands, you reply again. Someone sends a thumbs up, and somehow your brain files this under progress.

Meanwhile the one task that actually matters is still sitting there. Untouched. Quietly going bad, like a sad vegetable at the back of the fridge.
