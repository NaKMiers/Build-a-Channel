So why does everyone pretend to be busy?

Because modern life keeps confusing activity with value.

It rewards the person who looks available, fast, and overloaded. Even when the real work needs the opposite. Focus. Quiet. Time.

The problem was never that people are lazy. The problem is that looking busy became safer than looking thoughtful.

And once a whole culture rewards busy, everyone joins in. Students highlight notes instead of learning them. Workers answer messages instead of solving the problem. Creators organize their ideas instead of making the thing.

And we all say we are busy, because saying "I am protecting my attention from pointless noise" sounds far too honest for a Tuesday.

So maybe the goal is not to be less busy. The goal is to be more honest about what actually matters.

Because if everything is urgent, nothing is. And if your whole day is fake emergencies, congratulations. You are not lazy. You are just trapped in a calendar with Wi-Fi.
