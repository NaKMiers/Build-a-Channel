```text
The fourth reason is that "I'm busy" is genuinely useful. [beat] It is a [emphasis: shield]. [pause]

Sometimes it means, I cannot take on more. Sometimes it means, I do not want to make this decision. Sometimes it means, please stop asking me questions. [pause]

And sometimes it means, I have no idea what I am doing, but I need to look calm until the answer shows up. [pause]

This is not always dishonest. A lot of people really are [emphasis: overloaded]. The shield is real. [pause]

The problem is that "busy" slowly becomes the [emphasis: only] acceptable way to say no. [pause]

You cannot always say, I need quiet time to think. [beat] That sounds suspiciously relaxed. You cannot always say, this meeting could have been a message. [beat] That sounds dangerous. [pause]

So people just say, I'm busy. And everyone nods. Because everyone else is also busy. [beat] Or pretending. [beat] Or both.
```

- WIT here is protected and overwhelmed, not smug; `overloaded` corrects any "fake person" read.
