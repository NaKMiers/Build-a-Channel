The fourth reason is that "I'm busy" is genuinely useful. It is a shield.

Sometimes it means, I cannot take on more. Sometimes it means, I do not want to make this decision. Sometimes it means, please stop asking me questions.

And sometimes it means, I have no idea what I am doing, but I need to look calm until the answer shows up.

This is not always dishonest. A lot of people really are overloaded. The shield is real.

The problem is that "busy" slowly becomes the only acceptable way to say no.

You cannot always say, I need quiet time to think. That sounds suspiciously relaxed. You cannot always say, this meeting could have been a message. That sounds dangerous.

So people just say, I'm busy. And everyone nods. Because everyone else is also busy. Or pretending. Or both.
