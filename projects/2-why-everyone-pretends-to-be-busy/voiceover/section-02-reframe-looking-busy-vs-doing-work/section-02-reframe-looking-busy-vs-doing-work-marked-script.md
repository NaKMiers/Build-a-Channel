# Section 2 Marked Script - Reframe: Looking Busy vs Doing Work

Video: `Why Everyone Pretends To Be Busy`

Markup is for human voice direction only. It is stripped before TTS (hyperframes tts does not
consume these tags). Keep markup light per `script-learner-voice.md`.

```text
Now, this is not a video about lazy people hiding from work. That is a different, smaller problem. [pause]

This is about something stranger. Modern life often rewards the look of work more than the work itself. [beat]

Because real progress is hard to see. Thinking looks like nothing. Solving a problem in your head looks like nothing.

But busy is easy to see. You can see meetings. You can see fast typing. You can see a serious face staring at a screen.

So we trust the things we can see, and we quietly ignore the things we cannot. [beat] [slower] Which is a problem, because the real work usually hides in the part you cannot see.
```

Direction notes:
- `[pause]` after the "lazy people" line so the correction is clearly separated from the real point.
- `[beat]` before "Modern life often rewards the look of work..." to land the thesis.
- `[beat] [slower]` on the closing line (from the script's voice revision note) so the key idea lands clearly for learners.
- Keep delivery calm and lightly dry; the three short "looks like nothing" beats should feel matter-of-fact, not dramatic.
