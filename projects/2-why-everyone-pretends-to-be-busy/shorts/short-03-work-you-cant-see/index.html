THE LOOK OF WORK
THINKING =
LOOKS LIKE NOTHING
MEETINGS
FAST TYPING
A SERIOUS FACE

REAL WORK HIDES
WHERE YOU
CAN'T SEE IT

Modern life rewards
the look of work...
...more than the work itself.
Real progress is hard to see.
Thinking looks like nothing.
But busy is easy to see -
so we trust what we can see,
and ignore what we can't.
Which is a problem...
