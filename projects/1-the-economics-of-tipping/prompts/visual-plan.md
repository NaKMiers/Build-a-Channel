## Visual thesis

An ordinary coffee checkout progressively opens into a living compensation system. Every complete spoken sentence receives its own mini-plate, so the visual language keeps moving through intimate character action, evidence cards, mechanism diagrams, and expressive environmental reveals.

## Scene-density and continuity notes

The plan contains one prompt for every transcript cue and one fresh mini-plate for every completed spoken sentence. Variants only animate a plate while that sentence continues. New mini-plates stay inside the same chapter chain to retain paper texture, palette, and character identity. Generated text, exact figures, survey labels, and legal labels remain symbolic for later editing.
