# Visual plan - The Economics of Tipping

Visual style: HumanPrice current
Master brand: Toss blue #2E77C4

Recurring motif: the tip button expands into a receipt path, a wage ladder, and a fairness scale

## Visual thesis

An ordinary coffee checkout opens into a living compensation system. The terminal is the visible transaction, the receipt path reveals who names the cost, the wage ladder exposes the regulated mechanism, and the fairness scale makes the attention and autonomy cost visible. The ending deliberately returns to the hook composition and adds the complete system around the same unchanged coffee.

## Pacing and continuity notes

The plan contains 227 generated beats and 6 edit-only CAPCUT builds, for 233 meaningful visual states. The first 15 seconds reach 44 states per minute and the following 30 seconds reach 34 states per minute through simple terminal, eye-line, and receipt builds. Base plates stay within the preferred review band, every non-plate names one visible delta, and every variant repeats its source surface and camera task. Exact figures, legal labels, survey wording, and all load-bearing text remain symbolic for editing.

The seven macro build chains remain the narrative structure, while Flow inheritance resets at every independent base plate. The 98 generated PLATE rows create 97 chain breaks after the opening plate. VARIANT, CALLBACK, and CAPCUT rows remain connected to their source lineage.
