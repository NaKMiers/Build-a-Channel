## Description

Tipping looks voluntary, so why can pressing "No tip" feel like refusing part of the bill?
This episode follows one awkward checkout screen to reveal the wage system, social pressure, and hidden human cost behind the choice.

HumanPrice examines why ordinary money decisions can feel far bigger than the price on the screen. We look at the difference between a tip and a service charge, why checkout design changes the experience, and who is asked to name the final cost of service.
