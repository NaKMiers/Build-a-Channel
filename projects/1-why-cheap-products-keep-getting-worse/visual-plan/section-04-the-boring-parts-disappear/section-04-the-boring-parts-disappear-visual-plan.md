## Section Goal

Turn the abstract idea of "quality decline" into concrete, boring, durable parts — then show that because those parts are boring and hidden, they are the first thing cut. Land the recurring motif: the product still looks complete, it just has `less future built into it`.

## Visual Direction

- Big-scene/cue rhythm: `3 persistent big scenes, 6 cue states over 37.920s`
- Big scene rhythm: `boring materials (fabric/stitching) -> repairable mechanics (tools) -> the sealed box that hides it`
- Main idea: `the durable "future" of a product is boring parts; boring + hidden = first to be cut`
- WIT path: `bored/thinking at the boring list -> deadpan at the "printer person" joke -> betrayed at "less future built in"`
- WIT density: `3 beats; Scene 1: 1 (thinking, held cues 1-2), Scene 2: 1 (deadpan, cue 4), Scene 3: 1 (betrayed, cue 6); the parts-list cue and the "easy to hide" cue are WIT-free so the list/labels read`
- Motion density: `hard cuts between scenes; labels hard-show; the only timed reveals are the staggered parts list, the deadpan printer line, "EASY TO HIDE", and the payoff`
- Real-life texture: `3 real photo bases used clean — fabric/stitching, repair tools, a sealed cardboard box`
- Do-not: `do not itemise all 6 parts as 6 separate cuts; compress into a stacked list that builds one row per spoken item`
