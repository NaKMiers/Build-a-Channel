## Composition / Attention Notes

- Scene 1 opens on the *result* (a pile of discarded gadgets) so the viewer feels the waste before the explanation.
- Scene 2 reframes "the system" as a fulfillment line; the four friction reasons stagger in, with the 4th (the easy replacement path) highlighted green.
- Scene 3 pays off with a receipt that literally prints "AGAIN" three times, then the deadpan "subscription with extra steps".
