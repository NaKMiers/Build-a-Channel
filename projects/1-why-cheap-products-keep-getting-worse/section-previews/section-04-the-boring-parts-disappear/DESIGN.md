# DESIGN

Project:
`Why Cheap Products Keep Getting Worse`

Composition:
`Section04TheBoringPartsDisappear`

## Style Prompt

Simple funny explainer section about boring product future pieces disappearing. Use three large real photo backgrounds: fabric/stitching, screwdriver repair table, and cardboard/product box. Add only a few generic CSS overlays where needed. Keep labels handwritten, large, dry, and sparse. WIT appears huge on the opening inspection, printer aside, and final betrayal.

## Colors

- Paper background: `#f2eadb`
- Ink: `#17120f`
- Cream label: `#fff8df`
- Muted cardboard: `#c99b67`
- Repair blue gray: `#d8e7ee`
- Boring part gray: `#d9d1c3`
- Sale yellow: `#efc63d`
- Red markup: `#7d1714`
- Soft red label: `#fff1ea`
- Warm shadow: `rgba(42, 25, 12, 0.22)`

## Typography

- Handwritten labels: local `PatrickHand`, loaded from `assets/fonts/patrick-hand-latin.woff2`
- Keep text short, uppercase for labels, and large enough to read in 1080p screenshots.

## Motion

- Static hard cuts only.
- Ordinary cue labels hard-show on the spoken beat.
- Impact is reserved for the small `SECRET HANDSHAKE` cross-out and final `LESS FUTURE BUILT IN` stamp.
- No decorative transitions, no repeated fly-ins, and no MP4/WebM export unless explicitly requested.

## Timing

`whisper-derived` (remade 2026-06-21). Audio transcribed with `transformers.js` whisper-tiny.en (WASM); word timings saved to `voiceover/section-04-.../section-04-word-timings.json`. Scene cuts pinned to "a hinge" (`5.66`) and "most people see" (`26.92`); parts list reveals at fabric `3.9` / stitching `4.5` / hinge `5.66` / battery `7.62` / screw `9.46` / spare `12.72`; printer line `25.46`; easy-to-hide `33.3`; payoff `36.2`.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use the phone repair or printer repair photos directly.
- Do not show real device brands, model text, app UI, store logos, or real people.
- Do not draw, approximate, or CSS-build WIT.
- Do not crowd WIT into every cue.
- Do not place the final stamp on WIT's face or in the lower subtitle zone.
- Do not reintroduce the rejected parts-tray / many-card direction.
