# Section 4 Marked Script

The future of a product is usually not exciting. [pause]

It is boring things. [beat]

[slower] Thicker fabric.

[slower] Stronger stitching.

[slower] A hinge that does not act surprised by movement.

[slower] A battery you can replace.

[slower] A screw that uses a normal screwdriver instead of a secret handshake.

[slower] A spare part that still exists after the product leaves the factory. [pause]

These things are not very glamorous.

Nobody walks into a store and whispers, "Wow. Look at the long-term availability of replacement parts." [beat]

[deadpan] That person exists, but they are probably already repairing a printer. [beat]

Most people see the shape, the color, the price, and whether the product can arrive tomorrow. [pause]

So the boring future pieces are easy to hide.

The product still looks complete. [pause]

[slower] It just has less future built into it. [beat]
