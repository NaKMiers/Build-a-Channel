# Section 1 Marked Script

I find a chair for nine dollars. [pause]

It has four legs, a seat, and the confidence of a much better chair. [beat]

So he buys it.

For the first week, everything is fine. [pause]

[deadpan] Then the chair starts making a noise that sounds like legal advice. [beat]

The screw gets loose. The seat feels nervous. One leg begins exploring other career options. [pause]

And suddenly, the cheap chair was not really cheap. [pause]

It was a normal chair with one small problem:

[slower] future not included. [beat]
