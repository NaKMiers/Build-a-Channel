it is Toss in this video's costume.

Bottom-right: the prop panel above. Pure flat white background everywhere with no grid, no paper tone, no border and no frame around the sheet. Generous even whitespace around every figure; nothing crops or touches a divider.

RENDERING QUALITY: bold black outline of even, confident, slightly rounded weight throughout, clean and deliberate like a good vector marker pen, lively but never scratchy or wobbly. Flat solid color fills; one slightly darker tone of the same hue is allowed along the bottom edge of a filled shape to give it weight, nothing more. No gradients, no soft shading, no glow, no ambient occlusion, no paper or canvas texture. Crisp high-resolution linework, every panel sharp, no blur.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT — highest priority, overrides everything else: the image must contain zero written characters of any kind, not one letter, digit, word, symbol or glyph anywhere on the canvas. Do not label the panels: no FRONT, SIDE, BACK, three-quarter, no view names, no pose names, no expression names, no character name. Do not redesign between views. Side view is a true profile with one eye visible, the hair tuft reading from the side and the hoodie keeping its silhouette.
