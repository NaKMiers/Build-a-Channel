**Not given a sheet.** Kurt Lewin's waiter and Bluma Zeigarnik's laboratory subjects each appear in
a single moment, so they are one-off generic background figures per the scenes rules. The phone,
the open fridge, and the share of the kill are objects and live in their owner's prop panel.

**Why the ancestral cast is Arctic.** The script names exactly one culture and depicts it in
detail: the Greenlandic Inuit song duels from E. Adamson Hoebel. The earlier band-of-thirty
passage is culture-neutral ("a debt of meat", "the same fire", "a share of the kill"), so it
absorbs that setting without contradiction, and this keeps one consistent ancestral design
instead of spending two more cast slots on a second people. Gluckman's African societies are
cited in narration only and are never put on screen.
