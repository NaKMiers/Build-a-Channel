Derivation notes, written here so `scenes` does not re-litigate them:

- **`@FRIEND` is a locked entry, not a generic figure.** The person Toss advises bookends the
  whole video: the opening call, "somebody you love calls you in the dark" in the mismatch, and
  "be the friend on the other end" at the close. That is the highest drift exposure on the cast,
  hook plus ending, so it earns the lock over a one-section figure.
- **The phone is `@YOU`'s signature prop, not a cast entry.** The video is about the self-other
  split, not the device. The call and "the message your thumb is hovering over at midnight" ride
  in Toss's hands and get their lock on his sheet.
- **`@SOLOMON` is cast even though he appears in one section.** Same judgement as the Diderot
  figure in project 8: a single historical figure confined to the origin passage but drawn across roughly
  four contrasting beats inside it, the consultation, the judgment, the chaotic house and the
  cracking kingdom. That intra-section drift is enough to lock him.
- **The anthropology is Kalahari, not prehistoric.** The script names Richard B. Lee and the
  Ju/'hoansi and sets the section in a present-day forager camp. There is no caveman in this video.
- **`@YOU` wears a NEW outfit, as every video now requires.** A plain crew-neck sweatshirt in
  grass green `#3A9E3A`, no hood, over his charcoal `#3F3F46` shorts. Derived from the script's
  bookend room, where he is at home late at night with a phone in his hand. The cobalt hoodie in
  `brand/MASCOT.jpeg` is the identity reference only and is deliberately not carried forward. Green
  is unclaimed by this cast and holds against the coral `#D96F5F` modern ground.
- **No blue exception is needed in this cast.** Projects 2 and 3 both had to justify Toss and the
  ancestral group sharing a blue family. Here Toss is green, so `@CAMP`'s sky blue `#6EB5E8` is the
  only blue on the sheet and the group still gets its cool read against the tan camp ground.
- **Coral `#D96F5F` grounds the modern thread and scarlet `#D94040` is Solomon's robe.** They are
  both warm reds and must never share a frame. They cannot: coral only ever grounds the modern
  scenes and the robe only ever appears in the ancient naming section, grounded in lavender
  `#B79AD9` or tan `#C4965A`.
