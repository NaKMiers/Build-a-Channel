### Description

```text
Countries fight to host the World Cup. Then the receipt arrives.

This video explains why hosting the World Cup is sold like an investment, why the promised boom usually disappoints, and why countries still bid anyway.

The short version: the host gets the stadiums, security, transit, fan zones, and political pressure. FIFA gets the TV money, sponsor money, ticket money, and broad host-country tax exemptions. The World Cup makes money. Just not for the country holding it.

We look at Brazil 2014, South Africa 2010, Qatar 2022, and the 2026 USA / Mexico / Canada edition, where the hosts built zero new stadiums and still face major public costs.

Key idea for English learners:
"white elephant" = a big expensive thing you must keep paying for, even if you do not really need it.
```
