So why do countries fight to host the World Cup and lose billions?

They sell you an investment. They are buying something else. Attention. Status. A month of being the center of the world. Paid for with public money, planned by people who will not be in office when the receipt arrives.

Hosting is not a bad investment. It was never an investment. It is the world's most expensive selfie.

And the World Cup itself? Oh, it makes money. Every single time anyone has counted. For FIFA.

So next time someone promises your city a boom - a stadium, a big event, a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity - ask one simple question. Who keeps the tickets?

Because the party is fun. Just check whose name is on the receipt.
