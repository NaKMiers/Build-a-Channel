Then comes the contract. Before you host, you sign. Let us read it together.

FIFA keeps the TV money. FIFA keeps the sponsor money. FIFA keeps the ticket money. Feel free to spot the pattern.

The host pays for the stadiums. The security. The transport. The fan zones.

Oh, and guarantee number three: your government promises FIFA an exemption from taxes - meaning FIFA pays none. Not some taxes. All of them.

By the way, FIFA lives in Zurich, Switzerland. And legally, FIFA is a non-profit. It sits in the same legal category as a local chess club. A chess club with around four billion dollars in reserves.

The four years around the Qatar World Cup brought FIFA about seven and a half billion dollars in revenue. For the four years around this one, FIFA expects around thirteen billion. On track for its biggest payday ever.

To be fair, FIFA does pay for some things. Prize money. Running the tournament. What it does not pay for is your police, your trains, and your stadium.

So one side gets the revenue. The other side gets the bills. FIFA invented the only auction on Earth where the winner pays... and the auctioneer keeps the money.
