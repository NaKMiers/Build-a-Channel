Most people think hosting is an investment. Tourists arrive, hotels fill, shops get rich, the economy grows. That is the story every bidding government tells.

It is a lovely story. [beat] The numbers tell a different one.

So here is the truth. Hosting the World Cup is not an investment. It is a purchase.

Nobody buys a Ferrari to make money. You buy a Ferrari to be SEEN in a Ferrari.

Countries do the same. Except the Ferrari is a football tournament, the price is billions... [beat] and the credit card is yours. The taxpayer's.

So the real question is never "will it pay off". It is "who pays".
