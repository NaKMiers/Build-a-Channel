"But wait," says every tourism minister.
...
"The fans come! They spend real money!"
...
...
True. .
...
Fans do come. .
...
They do spend.
...
So where does the boom go?
...
...
...
Down three drains.
...
...

Drain one...
...
substitution.
...
Locals do not spend NEW money during the Cup.
...
They move money they already had.
...
The hundred dollars you spend on a match ticket...
...
is a hundred you do not spend at the cinema.
...
...
The city did not get richer. .
...
The money just changed seats.
...
...

Drain two...
...
crowding out.
...
Big event, big crowds, big prices.
...
So the normal tourists look at the chaos and say...
"maybe next year".
...
And locals hide at home.
...
...
Right now, in 2026...
some fan-zone streets are having record days...
...
while shops a few blocks away...
are down around twenty percent.
...
...
The party does not spread. .
...
It just moves money around.
...
...

Drain three...
...
leakage.
...
The money spent inside the stadium...
tickets, food, sponsor stuff...
...
does not stay in town.
...
It leaks.
...
...
...
Mostly toward Zurich.
...
...

So the party happens in your house.
...
...
...
And the money leaves with the guests.
