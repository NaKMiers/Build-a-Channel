# Section 5 Design - The Three Drains

Video: `Why Countries Fight to Host the World Cup (and Lose Billions)`
Section: `Section 5: The Three Drains` (55.851s, 7 scenes)

## Design intent

Concede the true half ("fans do come, they do spend" - green checks) then show WHERE the
boom goes: down three labeled drains. The ornate cast-iron grate is the section hero (one
file, CSS-labeled per drain); the party money pile is the running gag - it shrinks at every
drain scene (100% -> 70% -> 45% -> 20% fading) and by 5.7 the last banknotes walk out the
front door with suitcases.

## Color story

- Teal-cream parody money owns every money element (pile, bills, seated/flying/walking bills).
- Red = the squeeze: surge tags, -$100, down ~20%, 2026 stamp, X strokes.
- Green = the concession: TRUE stamp + checks only (5.1).
- The grates are the only cold black shapes in every scene they enter.

## Honesty rails on screen

- Both minister claims get REAL green checks before the drains open (concede-then-reframe).
- 2026 board keeps the script's hedges verbatim: `some fan-zone streets`, `down ~20%`, dated `2026` stamp.
- CITY TOTAL gauge settles on `same` - not richer, not poorer (no false "loss" claim).
- Zurich pays off S4's geography gloss via the signpost, no new claims.
