# Section 4 Design - FIFA Keeps The Money

Video: `Why Countries Fight to Host the World Cup (and Lose Billions)`
Section: `Section 4: FIFA Keeps The Money` (62.101s, 9 scenes)

## Design intent

Read FIFA's contract out loud and make the one-way money flow visible. GREEN arrows =
money to FIFA (left-to-right, ending at the gold safe). RED arrows = bills to the host
(right-to-left, ending at WIT). Green thickens as revenue grows; red never shrinks.
This scene grammar is BORN in this section and stays consistent for the whole video.

## Color story

- Green (#1d8a3e / #7dff9f labels) owns "money to FIFA": highlighter swipes, arrows, LED digits, safe glow, DOES-PAY chips.
- Red (#e0392b) owns "bills / the exemption / not-pay": red arrow, chip stripes, PAYS: $0 stamp, X sweeps, cross-out, bounced tags.
- Gold owns FIFA's side: safe, bars, sack, banner, trophy.

## Honesty rails on screen

- `PAYS: $0` stamps ONTO the contract strip (scoped to the guarantee, not a global tax claim).
- $7.5B is labeled REVENUE; $13B appears only after the plate flips to EXPECTS; sub-chips say "the four years around..." exactly as spoken.
- The fairness beat physically lands PRIZE MONEY / RUNNING THE TOURNAMENT chips on FIFA's paying side (asymmetry shown, not a zero).
- Receipt prints exactly `16x STADIUM (RETROFIT) ......... $???`.
