# Section 1 Hook - Design Notes

Video: `Why Countries Fight to Host the World Cup (and Lose Billions)`
Section: `Section 1: Hook: The Trophy Prints A Receipt` (35.904s, 7 scenes)

## Visual grammar

- Standing template: vivid real photo base per scene + pre-made isolated assets
  composited on top + giant varied WIT + sparse handwritten labels.
- Hero object: `trophy-gold-parody.png` appears in 5 of 7 scenes (video hero).
- Motif birth: `receipt-endless-roll.png` starts printing in 1.3 and returns in 1.6
  (pile) and 1.7 (shoulder drape) - it follows WIT out of the hook.
- Scene-type rotation: wide celebration -> object hero close-up -> wide gag/motif birth
  -> evidence board -> checklist device -> glamour reveal + pan -> mascot-only focus.
- Hard cuts only; scene boundaries pinned to whisper word starts.
