## Sections 4-9 (visual-implement, 2026-07-06)

session, so the bulk came from Wikimedia Commons + rawpixel (CC0) + Flickr (CC) + StockSnap (CC0). Every
file was Read-verified people-free / brand-free / watermark-free for its use. Substitutions and caveats
are recorded per row and mirrored in `asset-manifest.md` sourcing notes. `tv-wall-glow-1.jpg` (S4.2) was
NOT sourceable license-safe and is a generate fallback (manifest S4-G10) - no attribution row.
