Derivation notes, written here so `scenes` does not re-litigate them:

- **The phone is not a cast entry.** It is `@YOU`'s signature prop, locked on his sheet. The
  script is about the object that _lost_ value, not the one that arrived, so `@CASE` carries the
  lock and the phone rides along in Toss's hands.
- **The anthropology is Kalahari, not prehistoric.** The script names Richard B. Lee and the
  Ju/'hoansi explicitly and sets the whole section in a present-day forager camp. There is no
  caveman in this video.
- **`@YOU` wears a NEW outfit, as every video now requires.** A plain crew-neck sweatshirt in
  golden yellow `#F5C518`, no hood, over his charcoal `#3F3F46` shorts. Derived from the script's
  bookend room, where he is at his own desk in the evening with a newly unboxed phone. The cobalt
  hoodie from `brand/MASCOT.jpeg` is the identity reference only and is deliberately not carried
  forward. Yellow is unclaimed by this cast and holds up against the coral `#D96F5F` modern ground.
- **No blue exception is needed in this cast.** Projects 2 and 3 both had to justify Toss and the
  ancestral group sharing a blue family. Here Toss is yellow, so `@CAMP`'s sky blue `#6EB5E8` is
  the only blue on the sheet and the group still gets its cool read against the olive `#8FA35A`
  and tan camp grounds. Changing Toss's outfit dissolved a conflict two earlier videos had to
  work around.
- **Coral `#D96F5F` is the modern chapter tint and scarlet `#D94040` is Diderot's gown.** They are
  both warm reds and must never share a frame. They cannot: coral grounds the modern thread and the
  gown only ever appears in the 1769 thread, which is grounded in lilac `#B79AD9`.
