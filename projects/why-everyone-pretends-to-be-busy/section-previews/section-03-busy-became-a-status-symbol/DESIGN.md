## Idea
Reason 1: busy became a status symbol. "I'm so busy" signals importance; busy is treated like an
emotion; the brag flips by culture; busy *sounds* valuable, so people fill the day until the job
becomes proving you're near the work.

  about meetings" (calendar); CSS deadpan EMOTIONS board.
