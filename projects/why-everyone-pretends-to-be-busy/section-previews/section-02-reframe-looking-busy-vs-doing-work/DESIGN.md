## Idea
The reframe: it's not about lazy people. Modern life rewards the *look* of work over the work. Real
progress (thinking) is invisible; busy (meetings/typing/serious face) is visible — so we trust the
visible and ignore the real work that hides where we can't see.

## New standard applied (remade from scratch)
- Real photo base behind EVERY scene; REAL-UI (a Google Meet grid) for the "busy is easy to see" beat.
- WIT GIANT (~1/2 frame) and HIGH (head+torso inside frame, legs cropped); labels arranged around it.
