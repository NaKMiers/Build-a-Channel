The third reason is that visible work is just easier to reward.

Reply quickly, and people see it.
Join every meeting, and people see it.
Send an update that says, "just circling back," and people definitely see it.

But spend two hours thinking hard, and the result can look like nothing happened.
You might have solved the real problem.
From the outside, you were a person staring at a wall... possibly blinking.

There is even a survey where most managers admitted they could not really tell who on their team was being productive.
So they reward what they can see.

And what they can see is motion.
So people start performing motion.
They reply fast, they join the call, they move a task from one column to another column, and they write updates about future updates.

This has a name. Productivity theater.
Same as normal theater, except the tickets are paid in stress, and the star of the show is a spreadsheet.
