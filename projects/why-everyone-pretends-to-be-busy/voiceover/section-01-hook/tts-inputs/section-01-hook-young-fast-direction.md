# Section 1 Young Fast Voice Direction

Voice test target:
young male narrator, around age 22, bright and clear, not elder, not deep, not dramatic.

Local scratch voice:
`am_adam`

Speed target:
fast enough to land near `25s`, but still understandable for English learners.

Delivery:
dry, casual, lightly suspicious. Do not over-pause after every sentence. Keep the final line dry, not theatrical.
