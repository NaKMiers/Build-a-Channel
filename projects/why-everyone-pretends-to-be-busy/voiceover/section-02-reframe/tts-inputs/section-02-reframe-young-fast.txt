This video is not about lazy people pretending to work. That is a different problem.

This is about something stranger. Modern life often rewards the appearance of work more than the work itself.

Because real progress is hard to see. But busyness is very visible.

You can see meetings. You can see messages. You can see someone typing very fast with a serious face.

You cannot always see thinking. Which is unfortunate, because thinking is where a lot of the actual work happens.
