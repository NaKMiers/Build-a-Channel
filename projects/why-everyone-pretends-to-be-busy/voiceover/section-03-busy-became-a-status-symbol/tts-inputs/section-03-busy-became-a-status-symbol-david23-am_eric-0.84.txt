The first reason is simple. Busy became a status symbol.

When someone says "I'm so busy," it does not only mean they have a lot to do. It can also mean, please notice that I am important.

This is why people say it even when nobody asked. You say, how are you? They say, busy. Which is not actually an emotion. But we let it go, because everyone is tired.

And it is not the same everywhere. In some places, the proud thing is long holidays and free afternoons. In others, the proud thing is having no free time at all. Same planet, opposite brag.

Busy sounds responsible. Busy sounds needed. Busy sounds like people are lined up waiting for you. And being needed feels like being valuable.

So people fill the day. Sometimes with real work. Sometimes with calls, updates, and meetings about why the last meeting did not finish anything.

At some point the job stops being the work. The job becomes proving you are near the work.
