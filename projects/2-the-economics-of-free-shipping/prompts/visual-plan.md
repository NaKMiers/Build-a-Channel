# Visual plan - The Economics of Free Shipping

Visual style: HumanPrice current
Master brand: Toss blue #2E77C4
Chapter colors: Olive #6F7D3C, Terracotta #C86B3C, Muted gold #D5A84B
Recurring motif: one shipping line and the parcel it pays for, tracked by a thin blue money thread

## Visual thesis

A checkout shows one line for delivery, and that line is the only part of the system a shopper can price. When it drops to zero the cost does not leave the world, it moves: into the basket, into the price of the goods, into the fees a seller pays, and into the shelf price of a person who never shops online. Every act keeps the same parcel and the same thin blue thread visible, so the audience can watch a real cost travel while the label disappears. The ending returns to the opening cart and lets the same zero mean something different.

## Pacing and continuity notes

The plan contains 281 generated beats and 32 edit-only CAPCUT builds, for 313 meaningful visual states across a 10m14s recording.

Two deliberate departures from the usual review bands, both driven by the brief for this episode, which was that each frame stay simple and carry one idea, and that the cuts follow the voiceover exactly.

First, density is weighted toward CLEAN: 185 CLEAN, 96 LAYERED, 0 ATMOSPHERIC. That is 65 percent CLEAN against the usual forty percent guidance. ATMOSPHERIC sits at 0 percent, inside its ten percent ceiling: a richer environment is exactly the added complexity the brief asked to avoid, so no beat claims one.

Second, new base plates are 45 of 281 generated rows, about 16 percent, well under the thirty-five to forty-five percent review band. This is the direct cost of the same decision. Holding one composition and changing a single readable element per cue is what makes a frame simple and what makes it track a spoken clause precisely, so most cues are one-step builds rather than fresh scenes. The rhythm that produces is a new composition roughly every fourteen seconds with five or six build steps inside it. If a later review prefers more visual variety over per-cue simplicity, the place to spend it is C5, the long evidence act, where several build chains run six or more beats deep.

Seven macro chains carry the narrative. Flow inheritance is a separate structure: it resets at every independent base plate, so the 45 generated PLATE rows are protected by 44 chain breaks. 229 VARIANT rows and 7 CALLBACK rows stay wired to their lineage and are never broken from it. Every non-plate names one visible, information-bearing delta.

All load-bearing figures stay out of the generated images. The eleven dollar and one cent gap, the forty percent, the shipping and subscription totals, the returns figure, and the legal definition are all edit-only, because generated text is unreliable and these are the claims the episode is accountable for.
