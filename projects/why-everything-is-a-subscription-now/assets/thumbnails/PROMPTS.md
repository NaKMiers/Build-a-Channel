## The drama toolkit (baked into every prompt)

- **Extreme WIT face**: dot eyes blown into huge shocked circles, jaw dropped, eyebrows shot up, cartoon sweat drops, hands on cheeks/head. Push it to meme-level panic.
- **Tighter crop**: WIT bigger in frame (½ to ⅔), so the face/emotion dominates on a phone.
- **One shocking element**: a giant red number, a trap snapping, a flood of charges, a chain/lock.
- **Danger cues**: glowing red accents, motion/speed lines, small impact bursts, a hard drop shadow.
- **Real photo object + flat cartoon WIT** mix (the channel look). Background: one bold saturated color.
- Text stays **1–3 words**, huge bold handwritten. On-image words must NOT repeat the title.
