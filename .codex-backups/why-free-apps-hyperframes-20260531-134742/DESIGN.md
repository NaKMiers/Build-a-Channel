# Why Free Apps HyperFrames Design

## Style Prompt

Create a simple board-based no-face explainer for `Why It Works`: warm paper background, charcoal handwritten text, red correction marks, yellow and teal label cards, rough underlines, WIT as a deadpan reaction anchor, and clean scene transitions. The video should feel human, funny, and easy to follow for English learners. The look should be closer to a clear hand-drawn explainer board than a polished SaaS animation.

## Colors

- Paper: `#fffdf7` for the main canvas
- Ink: `#101827` for text and outlines
- Red Markup: `#e5162e` for crosses, warnings, and punch emphasis
- Yellow Note: `#f2c14e` for `FREE`, pricing, and app labels
- Teal Note: `#8dd6df` for systems, attention, and behavior labels
- Soft Panel: `#f7f1e8` for cards and board shadows

## Typography

- Primary handwritten stack: `Patrick Hand`, `Comic Neue`, cursive
- Fallback system stack: `Segoe UI`, Arial, sans-serif
- Use large, short labels. Avoid paragraph-length text on screen.

## Motion Rules

- Use board-to-board transitions through paper sweeps, soft wipes, or quick crossfades.
- Every scene gets entrance animation for text, WIT, and cards.
- Keep motion readable: pop cards on spoken cues, draw underlines on emphasis, and avoid distracting loops.
- No real app logos. Use generic phone, app cards, labels, and WIT poses.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use dense slide-deck layouts.
- Do not use generic tech gradients or glossy 3D.
- Do not introduce real app logos into the rough cut.
- Do not over-animate WIT; the topic stays first.
- Do not let text overflow cards or sit flush against frame edges.
