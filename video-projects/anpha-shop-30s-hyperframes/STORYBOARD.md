# Storyboard

**Format:** 1920x1080 landscape
**Duration target:** 30 seconds
**Audio:** Edge TTS Vietnamese female voiceover (`vi-VN-HoaiMyNeural`). Underscore and SFX can be added in a later polish pass.
**VO direction:** Vietnamese female, friendly and practical product-ad delivery; calm but quick enough for a 30-second storefront promo
**Style basis:** DESIGN.md, captured Anpha Shop colors, Montserrat/Source Sans Pro, real product assets from `capture/assets/`

## Global Direction

This is a compact storefront promo, not an explainer. The video should feel like a fast tour through a dark e-commerce dashboard: cyan corner accents, purple CTA flashes, white product cards sliding through the frame, and real category/product art doing most of the selling. Each beat should have at least one active motion system: carousel drift, card cascade, counter rise, SVG line draw, or kinetic type.

## Beat 1 - Hook / Premium Without Premium Prices (0.00-3.70s)

**VO cue:** "Need premium accounts without paying premium prices?"

**Concept:** The viewer lands inside Anpha Shop's dark storefront. The logo snaps in first, then the hero image opens like a store window while cyan corner shapes frame the screen.

**Visual description:** Charcoal background with giant cyan diagonal corners. A dark nav bar assembles across the top. The Anpha logo pulses on the left, the search box stretches open, and the hero image `thot-khi-tn-th.jpg` slides into a large rounded white frame. The hook appears as kinetic type: "PREMIUM ACCOUNTS" then "WITHOUT PREMIUM PRICES?"

**Assets:** `favicon.png`, `thot-khi-tn-th.jpg`

**Animation choreography:** Logo pops with a tiny rotation. Search bar draws from left to right. Hero panel slides up and slow-zooms. Hook words cascade in with slight horizontal decay. Cyan diagonal accents drift slowly.

**Transition:** Blur-through into Beat 2.

## Beat 2 - One Storefront, Many Categories (3.70-11.90s)

**VO cue:** "Anpha Shop puts Netflix, YouTube Premium, Spotify, Grammarly, Office, CapCut, Cursor AI, and design tools in one fast storefront."

**Concept:** Product categories become a fast moving carousel wall. The frame should feel packed but controlled, like a store shelf moving toward the viewer.

**Visual description:** Seven product cards orbit through a shallow 3D carousel. Category chips for Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, Grammarly, Office, CapCut, Cursor AI, and Design App sweep across the bottom. White cards keep the real storefront style; cyan and purple badges mark them as active.

**Assets:** Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, Grammarly, Office, CapCut, Cursor AI product images.

**Animation choreography:** Cards cascade in from alternating sides, settle into a perspective shelf, then drift horizontally. Category chips type on in sync with the voice. Thin cyan SVG connector lines draw between the logo and the product shelf.

**Transition:** Velocity-matched upward push into Beat 3.

## Beat 3 - Top Deals / Compare Plans (11.90-14.80s)

**VO cue:** "Browse the top deals. Compare short plans."

**Concept:** The storefront becomes a focused comparison board: prices, durations, and sold counts become readable hero elements.

**Visual description:** Three large cards fill the frame: Netflix one week, Grammarly seven days, and Spotify one month. Each card has an enlarged price chip, a sold-count strip, and a purple `MUA NGAY` button. A `Top #10` title locks to the upper-left.

**Assets:** Netflix, Grammarly, Spotify product images.

**Animation choreography:** Cards drop in with staggered bounce. Price chips count upward from zero. The `Top #10` text stamps in. Buttons glow once in purple.

**Transition:** Quick zoom-through into Beat 4.

## Beat 4 - Proof / Discounts (14.80-20.70s)

**VO cue:** "See sold counts, flash sales, and discounts from twenty to ninety percent off the original price."

**Concept:** This is the proof beat. Numbers become the motion engine: sold counts climb, discount badges flip, and the 20-90% range dominates the frame.

**Visual description:** A wall of smaller product cards slides behind a giant centered `20%-90%` discount range. Orange/yellow deal chips pop over card corners. Sold counters appear as small ticker panels with tabular digits.

**Assets:** Product card images from Netflix, Office, YouTube, Grammarly, Spotify.

**Animation choreography:** Counter numbers roll upward. Discount badges flip on a Y-axis. Background cards pan slowly diagonally. The `20%-90%` headline enters with a purple-to-cyan glow pulse.

**Transition:** Diagonal cyan wipe into Beat 5.

## Beat 5 - Trust / Buy Fast (20.70-24.40s)

**VO cue:** "Then buy in a few clicks with instant payment and reliable warranty support."

**Concept:** The noisy product shelf resolves into four trust cards. The beat should feel reassuring and practical.

**Visual description:** Four white trust cards line up across the center: diverse products, cheapest market, instant payment, reliable warranty. A simple checkout path draws from left to right, ending in a glowing purple `MUA NGAY` button.

**Assets:** `a-dng-sn-phm.jpg`, `r-nht-th-trng.jpg`, `thanh-ton-lp-tc.jpg`, `bo-hnh-uy-tn.jpg`

**Animation choreography:** Cards slide in one by one. SVG path draws across the cards. The checkout button presses down once. Each card has subtle floating motion.

**Transition:** Soft overexposure flash into Beat 6.

## Beat 6 - CTA / Brand Close (24.40-30.00s)

**VO cue:** "Anpha Shop. Premium digital tools, easier to find, easier to buy."

**Concept:** The final frame returns to the logo and the strongest promise. The storefront assets compress into one clean brand shelf.

**Visual description:** Logo large on left, product cards fanned on right, dark nav shelf behind them. The line "Premium digital tools" appears first, then "easier to find, easier to buy." A cyan underline draws under the final phrase.

**Assets:** `favicon.png`, selected product cards.

**Animation choreography:** Logo scales in with a soft cyan glow. Product cards fan out with 3D rotation. Final line appears with per-word kinetic type. Underline draws in cyan, CTA button glows purple, then everything holds for readability.

**Transition:** Final hold, no fade until after 30 seconds.
