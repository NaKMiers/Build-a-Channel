# Design System

## Overview

Anpha Shop is a dark, discount-forward e-commerce storefront for premium digital accounts and software services. The page uses a charcoal background, a fixed dark navigation bar, white card surfaces, and sharp cyan/purple brand accents. The layout is dense and product-led: side category filters, a large cinematic hero banner, carousel-like product rows, discount badges, price cards, and repeated `MUA NGAY` buttons. The overall feel is fast, practical, and deal-focused rather than luxury or minimal.

## Colors

- **Page Background**: `#333333` - charcoal canvas used across the storefront.
- **Header Surface**: `#2F2E3E` - dark navy-purple navigation and footer panels.
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - product cards, filters, and trust blocks.
- **Muted Border**: `#E5E7EB` - pale dividers and card boundaries.
- **Brand Cyan**: `#01DBE5` - logo accent, recharge button, small action accents.
- **CTA Purple**: `#7655E6` - login and strong call-to-action emphasis.
- **Blue Accent**: `#0EA5E9` - secondary blue highlight for small UI marks.
- **Deep Blue Accent**: `#0284C7` - darker blue for supporting detail and depth.
- **Text Dark**: `#333333` - primary text on white cards.
- **Black Detail**: `#000000` - icons, dense labels, and high-contrast detail.

## Typography

- **Primary Font**: Montserrat (400, 600, 700). Used for headings, brand marks, product card names, prices, and CTAs.
- **Secondary Font**: Source Sans Pro (400, 600, 700). Used for body copy, product descriptions, smaller labels, and long supporting text.
- **Hierarchy**: Section headings sit around 36px on the site; product names use compact 16px semibold text. For video, preserve the compact commerce feel but scale up: 76-124px display lines, 28-42px supporting copy, and 20px+ labels.
- **Numeric Style**: Prices, discounts, and sold counts should use tabular numbers and strong weight contrast.

## Elevation

Depth comes from white product cards sitting on a dark charcoal canvas, with subtle borders, soft shadows, and rounded storefront containers. The site does not use heavy glassmorphism. In video, use layered cards, carousel stacks, device-like panels, and cyan/purple glow accents instead of large generic drop shadows.

## Components

- **Fixed Storefront Nav**: Dark `#2F2E3E` top bar with the AnphaShop logo, cyan recharge button, search box, cart icon, and purple login button.
- **Cinematic Hero Slider**: Large central media panel flanked by white category/filter sidebars.
- **Horizontal Product Carousel**: Dense white cards with product art, title, price, sold count, discount badge, and purple/cyan buy button treatment.
- **Category Rails**: Small label tabs for Netflix, CapCut, Design App, Cursor AI, YouTube, Grammarly, Spotify, and Microsoft Office.
- **Deal Badges**: Yellow/orange discount chips and flash-sale labels attached to product cards.
- **About Panel**: White content panel with service explanation and product imagery.
- **Trust Illustration Row**: Four compact cards: diverse products, cheapest market, instant payment, and reliable warranty.
- **Dark Footer Shelf**: Dense footer with logo, product links, account links, feature links, and social icons.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use the real dark storefront colors `#333333` and `#2F2E3E`.
- Keep cyan `#01DBE5` and purple `#7655E6` as the main motion accents.
- Show actual product imagery generously: Netflix, Office, YouTube, Grammarly, Spotify, CapCut, and Cursor AI.
- Use card stacks, carousel movement, and deal badges to preserve the e-commerce identity.
- Make price and discount moments large enough to read in motion.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the brand into a clean SaaS landing page; the storefront is dense and commerce-first.
- Do not use generic stock gradients as the main visual identity.
- Do not replace the captured logo/product art with invented icons.
- Do not make text-only beats for more than one beat in a row.
- Do not use tiny web-sized product text; the video must remain readable at 1080p.
