# 08 Section 01 Simple Remake

Video:
`Why Everyone Pretends To Be Busy`

Section:
`01 Hook`

Status:
`simplified Casually Explained-style remake`

## Reason

The previous Section 1 pass had too many cue-level pop-ins, transitions, and micro-animations for a `24s` hook.

New direction:

```text
static real-life board -> WIT reaction -> short handwritten label -> hard cut
```

No transition overlays.
No object-by-object animation.
No WIT shake.
No disappearing labels inside a board.

## Simplification Rules

- Background changes happen as hard cuts only.
- Same-background beats still use hard cuts, but no transition overlay.
- Every board is readable as a paused frame.
- Labels are visible at board start, not animated word-by-word.
- Keep real-life images as evidence, but do not turn them into moving collage layers.
