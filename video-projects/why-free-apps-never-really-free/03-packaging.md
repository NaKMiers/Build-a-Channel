# 03 Packaging

## Recommended Title

`Why Free Apps Are Never Really Free`

## Backup Titles

1. `The Real Price of Free Apps`
2. `Why “Free” Apps Cost More Than You Think`
3. `The Hidden Checkout Inside Free Apps`
4. `How Free Apps Actually Make Money`

## Thumbnail Direction

Concept: `Hidden Checkout`

Visual:

- phone screen with a big `FREE` button
- hidden checkout, receipt, or payment machine behind the free button
- Wit staring at the phone with a deadpan suspicious expression
- receipt trail coming out of the phone

## Thumbnail Text

`FREE?`

## First 5-Second Promise

`Some of the most powerful companies on Earth keep giving you useful things for free. Which is either a beautiful example of human progress... or a very suspicious business plan.`

## Final Packaging Decision

- Title: `Why Free Apps Are Never Really Free`
- Thumbnail text: `FREE?`
- Thumbnail concept: hidden checkout inside a free app
- Hook: suspiciously generous free apps
