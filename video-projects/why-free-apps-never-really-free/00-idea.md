# 00 Idea

## Raw Idea

`Why Free Apps Never Really Free?`

## Clean Angle

`Why Free Apps Are Never Really Free`

## Viewer Curiosity

Most viewers use free apps every day and vaguely know those apps make money somehow.
The stronger curiosity is not `do free apps make money?`
It is:

`Where did the payment go if the app did not ask for money upfront?`

## Channel Fit

- Lane: `internet behavior + business + modern life`
- Tone fit: `smart, simple, funny, dry`
- Why it works: it turns a normal internet habit into a hidden-system explainer

## Scorecard

| Criteria | Score | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Brand fit | 9/10 | Strong fit for money, internet, business, and modern life. |
| Curiosity | 8/10 | Familiar topic, but the hidden checkout angle makes it sharper. |
| Clarity | 9/10 | Easy promise: explain the hidden cost of free. |
| Relatability | 10/10 | Almost everyone uses free apps. |
| Thumbnail potential | 9/10 | `FREE?`, app button, hidden checkout, receipt trail. |
| Production difficulty | 7/10 | Needs good visual metaphors, but not complex animation. |
| Humor potential | 8/10 | Strong jokes around ads, streaks, subscriptions, and free being suspicious. |
| Replay value | 7/10 | Strong if the script has memorable lines. |

## Decision

`Go`

## Main Risk

The video can become too obvious if it only says:

`Free apps make money from ads and data.`

The stronger angle is:

`Free is not the absence of payment. It is the removal of friction.`
