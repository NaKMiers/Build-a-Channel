So how do free apps charge you?

The first way is obvious: ads.

If you are watching videos, scrolling feeds, or using a free platform, there is a good chance the product is not exactly the app.

The product is your attention.

The app gets people in, keeps them there, then sells pieces of that attention.

That is why free apps care so much about retention.

Not because they love you.

Because every extra minute is inventory.

Your boredom is inventory.
Your curiosity is inventory.
Your plan to check one notification and somehow lose twenty minutes is also inventory.

A tragic but very scalable business model.
