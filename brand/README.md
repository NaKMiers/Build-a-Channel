- `logo.png`: icon-only HumanPrice avatar, 1024 x 1024.

## Brand system

- Channel name: HumanPrice
- Core promise: Every human behavior has a price.
- Permanent cross-channel color: Toss blue `#2E77C4`
- HumanPrice system accent: Olive `#6F7D3C`
- HumanPrice cost accent: Terracotta `#C86B3C`
- Supporting colors: warm cream and charcoal

Toss blue remains the main color of the shared warm-doodle visual style. Niche palettes may
add meaning but never replace it inside characters, scenes, diagrams, and thumbnails. This
rule does not require the existing logo or banner to be regenerated.

## Channel description

HumanPrice reveals the economics hidden inside ordinary human behavior. From tipping and
dating to Black Friday and free-to-play games, each episode follows the money, incentives,
and psychology behind choices that feel personal but are shaped by larger systems.

We break down visible prices, hidden costs, consumer psychology, and the time, attention,
autonomy, and opportunity people trade without noticing. New cinematic explainers turn
behavioral economics into stories you can see in your own life.
